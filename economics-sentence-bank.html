    You already know the economics. This page gives you the sentences. Most diagram answers use the same few patterns with different names in them.

    Practice mode blurs the English. Click a line to check yourself.

    The three-sentence frame

    Almost every diagram answer in this course tells the same short story. If you can write these three sentences, you already have a passing answer. Add the “because” part and it becomes a good one.

    1 · What happened, and who it affects[Event] raises / lowers [buyers’ willingness to pay / sellers’ costs], so the demand / supply for [good] increases / decreases.先講發生什麼事、影響到買方還是賣方。
    2 · How the curve movesThe demand / supply curve shifts right / left from D1 to D2.再講哪一條線、往哪邊移。
    3 · The result, and whyThe equilibrium price rises / falls from P1 to P2, and the equilibrium quantity rises / falls from Q1 to Q2, because …最後講價格、數量的結果，加一句原因。
    ExampleDurian becomes popular on Chinese social media, so the demand for Thai durian increases. The demand curve shifts right from D1 to D2. The equilibrium price rises from P1 to P2 and the quantity rises from Q1 to Q2. Because new trees take about five years to bear fruit, supply is inelastic in the short run, so the price rises a lot while the quantity rises only a little.榴槤在中國社群爆紅 → 需求右移 → 價格、數量都上升；因為新樹要五年才結果，短期供給沒彈性，所以價格漲很多、數量只多一點。

    圖上的標籤（D1、D2、P1、P2、Q1、Q2）直接寫進句子，英文就簡單很多，改卷的人也一眼看得出你的圖和文字是同一個故事。

    Shifters in English

    The first sentence is where most people get stuck, because you have to name the reason. These are the usual ones. Learn the English phrase, not a definition.

    中文的意思Demand side: say it like this
    所得增加Higher incomes increase the demand for a normal good.
    替代品變貴Coffee becomes more expensive, so some buyers switch to tea, a substitute. The demand for tea increases.
    互補品變便宜Cheaper printers increase the demand for ink, a complement.
    流行、喜好改變The product becomes popular / fashionable, so the demand increases.
    預期未來漲價Buyers expect the price to rise next month, so they buy more now.
    買的人變多More tourists arrive, so the number of buyers increases.
    新用途A new use for the product raises buyers’ willingness to pay at every price.
    中文的意思Supply side: say it like this
    原料、工資變貴Higher fertilizer prices raise production costs, so the supply decreases.
    技術進步New technology lowers the cost of production, so the supply increases.
    天災、乾旱A drought cuts the harvest, so the supply decreases.
    賣的人變多New firms enter the market, so the supply increases.
    禁止出口（從世界市場看）The export ban removes a large share of rice from the world market, so world supply decreases.
    土地改種別的High durian prices raise the opportunity cost of using land for rubber, so the supply of rubber decreases.
    課稅 / 補貼A tax raises the cost of selling each unit, so the supply curve shifts up (left). A subsidy does the opposite.
    Watch this one“Demand increases” means the whole curve shifts. “Quantity demanded increases” means a move along the curve because the price fell.如果是價格本身變了，要寫 quantity demanded / quantity supplied，線不會動。
    ExampleThe higher price reduces the quantity demanded. The demand curve itself does not move.價格上升 → 需求量減少（沿著線移動），需求線本身沒動。

    When both curves move

    PatternBoth shifts push the price up, so the price rises for certain. The demand shift raises quantity while the supply shift lowers it, so the change in quantity is ambiguous: it depends on which shift is larger. In my diagram the demand shift is larger, so quantity rises.兩條線一起動：一個結果確定，另一個不確定。說清楚哪個確定、哪個看誰移得多，再說你的圖畫哪一種。

    Elasticity

    Elasticity usually comes as one extra sentence after the shift. It explains why the price moved a lot or a little.

    Why inelasticDemand for rice is inelastic because it is a necessity with few close substitutes, so buyers keep buying almost the same amount.必需品、沒什麼替代品、占預算比例小 → 缺乏彈性。
    Short run vs long runSupply is inelastic in the short run because new trees take five years to grow. In the long run, supply becomes more elastic.短期供給調整不了，長期才調整得了。
    What it does to the resultBecause demand is inelastic, the price has to rise a lot to clear the market, while the quantity falls only a little.缺乏彈性 → 價格變動大、數量變動小。
    Total revenueWhen demand is inelastic, a higher price increases total revenue, because the percentage rise in price is larger than the percentage fall in quantity.缺乏彈性時漲價，總收入增加；有彈性時漲價，總收入減少。
    Words that mean “elastic”Words that mean “inelastic”
    luxury, many close substitutes, large share of the budget, long time to adjustnecessity, few substitutes, small share of the budget, short run

    Price floors and ceilings

    Price floorThe minimum price is set above the equilibrium price, so it is binding. Sellers want to supply Qs but buyers only want Qd, so there is a surplus of Qs − Qd.價格下限設在均衡價格以上才有效，結果是過剩。
    Price ceilingThe maximum price is set below the equilibrium price, so buyers want Qd but sellers only supply Qs. There is a shortage of Qd − Qs.價格上限設在均衡以下，結果是短缺。
    Deadweight lossOnly Qd is traded, which is less than Qe, so some trades that would benefit both buyers and sellers do not happen. This lost gain is the deadweight loss.成交量少於均衡量，有些雙方都會得利的交易沒發生，這塊損失就是 DWL。

    Externalities

    Name it[Activity] harms [third party], who is not part of the trade. This is a negative externality, so the marginal social cost (MSC) lies above the private supply curve (S = MPC).先講誰被傷害、他不是交易的一方，再說 MSC 在 MPC 上面。
    Market vs efficientThe market produces at Qm, where demand meets private cost. The efficient quantity is Qe, where demand meets MSC. Because Qm > Qe, the market produces too much at too low a price, and the triangle between them is the deadweight loss.市場只看私人成本，所以做太多、賣太便宜。
    Positive externalityVaccination also protects other people, so the marginal social benefit (MSB) lies above demand. The market produces too little.正外部性反過來：MSB 在需求線上面，市場做太少。
    Why people ignore itEach driver ignores the delay they cause to other drivers, so too many people drive and roads become congested.新加坡塞車的例子：每個人只算自己的成本。
    PolicySay it like this
    Pigouvian taxA tax equal to the external cost makes producers pay the full social cost. Supply shifts up to S + tax, so output falls to Qe.
    Ban / regulationA ban is simple and direct, but it costs some firms much more than others and is hard to enforce.
    Coase bargainingBargaining is unlikely to work here, because millions of people are affected and transaction costs are very high.

    Firm decisions

    The professor said this question has no calculations. It asks what a firm should do when a cost or the price changes. The English is mostly about which cost matters.

    CostSay it like this
    Fixed costThe rent is a fixed cost: it does not change with output, so it does not affect how much to produce.
    Sunk costThe dryer bought last year is a sunk cost. It cannot be recovered, so it should not affect the decision.
    Variable costWages and fuel are variable costs. They make up the marginal cost of one more unit.
    Marginal costMarginal cost is the extra cost of producing one more unit.
    Competitive firmA small firm is a price taker, so its marginal revenue equals the market price. It should keep producing while price is at least equal to marginal cost (P ≥ MC).小廠商：價格 = MR，生產到 P = MC。
    When cost changesWhen the wage rises, MC shifts up from MC1 to MC2, so the profit-maximizing output falls from q1 to q2.變動成本上升 → MC 上移 → 產量減少。
    MonopolyA monopoly produces where marginal revenue equals marginal cost (MR = MC) and charges the price read from the demand curve.獨占：MR = MC 決定數量，價格從需求線讀。
    Fixed cost onlyIf only the rent rises, that is a fixed cost. MR and MC do not move, so price and quantity stay the same; only profit falls.只有固定成本變：數量價格都不變，只有利潤變少。

    Information problems

    First decide which problem it is. Ask: is the hidden thing the quality before the deal, or the behavior after the deal?

    交易前、看不出品質 → adverse selection（逆選擇）。簽約後、看不到對方行為 → moral hazard（道德風險）。

    Adverse selectionBefore the sale, sellers know the quality but buyers do not, so quality is a hidden type. Buyers offer only an average price. This is too low for sellers of good products, so they leave the market and average quality falls.買方只肯出平均價 → 好貨的賣家退出 → 平均品質越來越差。
    Moral hazardAfter the contract, the agent’s behavior cannot be fully watched, so it is a hidden action. The agent takes less care, and the principal bears the cost.簽約後對方行為看不到 → 比較不小心，損失由委託人承擔。
    The link to market failureBecause of asymmetric information, some trades that would benefit both sides do not happen. This is a market failure with a deadweight loss.資訊不對稱 → 有益的交易沒發生 → 市場失靈。
    ProblemFixSay it like this
    Adverse selectionScreeningThe buyer can screen, for example by testing a random sample of each shipment.
    Adverse selectionSignalingHonest sellers can signal quality with a certificate. It works only because low-quality sellers would fail the test.
    Moral hazardMonitoringThe government can monitor with frequent, unannounced audits.
    Moral hazardIncentivesA deposit returned only if the rice comes back at the same grade gives warehouses a reason to take care.

    Small English traps

    Not thisWrite this
    The price is rise.The price rises. / There is a rise in price.
    The tax rises costs.The tax raises costs. (raise + something; rise on its own)
    Demand increase.Demand increases.
    The curve move to right.The curve shifts to the right.
    Increase the price of 10 baht.Increase the price by 10 baht / to 60 baht.
    Less cars.Fewer cars (can count) · less traffic (cannot count)
    It effects the price.It affects the price. / It has an effect on the price.
    Because the drought. Supply falls.Because of the drought, supply falls. / Supply falls because the drought cut the harvest.

    Linking words you will use again and again: because, so, as a result, therefore, at the same time, however, assuming that, in the short run, in the long run. Write the whole story in the present simple (“shifts”, “rises”).

    because 後面接句子（because the drought cut the harvest），because of 後面接名詞（because of the drought）。整段用現在式就好，不用煩惱時態。

    Drill: Chinese to English

    Turn on Practice mode, write each answer on paper, then click the blurred line to check.

    1. 乾旱讓咖啡豆收成減少，咖啡豆市場會怎樣？A drought cuts the coffee harvest, so the supply of coffee beans decreases. The supply curve shifts left from S1 to S2. The equilibrium price rises from P1 to P2 and the quantity falls from Q1 to Q2.
    2. 咖啡變貴，茶的市場會怎樣？Tea is a substitute for coffee. When coffee becomes more expensive, some buyers switch to tea, so the demand for tea increases. The demand curve shifts right, and both the price and the quantity of tea rise.
    3. 米是必需品，供給減少時，價格和總收入會怎樣？Rice is a necessity with few substitutes, so demand is inelastic. When supply falls, the price rises a lot while the quantity falls only a little. Because the percentage rise in price is larger than the fall in quantity, total revenue increases.
    4. 工廠把廢水排進河裡，下游漁民受害。The factory’s waste harms fishermen downstream, who are not part of the trade. This is a negative externality, so MSC lies above the private supply curve. The market produces more than the efficient quantity, which creates a deadweight loss.
    5. 二手車買家看不出車況好壞。This is adverse selection. Sellers know the car’s quality but buyers do not, so buyers offer only an average price. Owners of good cars leave the market and average quality falls. A warranty or an independent inspection can signal or screen quality.
    6. 買了全險之後，駕駛開車變得比較不小心。This is moral hazard. After buying insurance, the driver’s care cannot be fully watched, so they take less care and the insurer bears the cost. A deductible or a no-claims discount gives drivers an incentive to stay careful.
    7. 工資上漲，小型競爭廠商該怎麼做？The wage is a variable cost, so marginal cost shifts up from MC1 to MC2. The firm is a price taker and produces where P = MC, so its profit-maximizing output falls from q1 to q2.

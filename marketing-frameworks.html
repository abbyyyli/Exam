    5Cs: Company, Customers, Competitors, Collaborators, ContextS1 · S5a

    The professor’s way to understand a situation before choosing: what the company has, who the customers are and what they want, who else competes, who helps deliver, and what is happening around them.老師分析現況的方法，在做選擇之前先看：公司有什麼、顧客是誰要什麼、還有誰在競爭、誰幫忙交付、周邊環境發生什麼。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： compares markets or countries, or asks you to analyse a situation before deciding比較市場或國家，或要你先分析情況再做決定 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Describing one market only; or mixing 5Cs and SWOT in one table只描述一個市場；或把 5C 與 SWOT 混在同一張表

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Company: strengths, resources, existing stores or products, capabilities.公司：優勢、資源、現有據點或產品、能力。
    • Customers: who they are and what they want – S1’s market size, age and income sit here; needs, wants and demand (wants backed by buying power).顧客：是誰、想要什麼——S1 的市場規模、年齡、所得放這裡；需要、想要、需求（有購買力的想要）。
    • Competitors: direct (same category) and indirect (other ways to meet the same need).競爭者：直接（同品類）與間接（滿足同一需要的其他方式）。
    • Collaborators: suppliers, distributors, retailers, partners, parent group businesses.合作者：供應商、通路、零售商、夥伴、母集團事業。
    • Context: economy and growth, regulation and politics, culture and religion, technology (the macro-environment of S4a).情境：經濟與成長、法規與政治、文化與宗教、科技（即 S4a 的總體環境）。

    5Cs vs SWOT: the same facts in two layouts. Company → Strengths and Weaknesses; Customers, Competitors, Collaborators and Context → Opportunities and Threats. Use SWOT when the question asks for it; otherwise a 5C contrast table is clearer for comparing two markets.5C 與 SWOT：同一批事實的兩種排法。公司 → 優勢與劣勢；顧客、競爭者、合作者、情境 → 機會與威脅。題目要 SWOT 就用 SWOT；比較兩個市場時，5C 對比表比較清楚。

    Where it leads: 5Cs (understand) → STP (choose whom to serve and why they choose us) → 4Ps (deliver). Slides: S1 “The 5Cs, STP and the 4Ps constitute the marketing management framework”; S5a “Marketing Overview”.接下來：5C（了解）→ STP（選擇服務誰、為何選我們）→ 4P（執行）。講義：S1「5C、STP、4P 構成行銷管理架構」；S5a「Marketing Overview」。

    How to use it in an answer: make one row per C with both markets side by side, mark which market wins each row, then turn the rows that matter into your reasons.答題用法：每個 C 一列、兩個市場並排，標出每列誰佔優，再把關鍵的列變成理由。

    4Ps (marketing mix)S1 · S4a · S5a

    The controllable tools – Product, Price, Place, Promotion – that deliver and communicate the chosen position to the target.可控制的工具——產品、價格、通路、推廣——把選定的定位傳遞並溝通給目標客群。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks how to implement a strategy, launch a product or reach a target問如何執行策略、推出產品或接觸目標客群 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： A generic 4P list that does not fit the target or the position泛泛的 4P 清單，沒有配合目標客群與定位

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Product: features, functions and benefits; how customers use it; how it differs from competitors; branding and positioning.產品：功能與利益、顧客怎麼用、和對手有何不同、品牌與定位。
    • Price: perceived value; compared with the market average and competitors; a pricing structure that fits the business model.價格：感知價值；和市場平均及對手比較；符合商業模式的定價結構。
    • Place: how target customers prefer to shop; where they find rivals; channels that are seamless and convenient; new channels.通路：目標顧客偏好怎麼買、在哪裡找到對手、無縫便利的通路、新通路。
    • Promotion: which segments need engagement; where customers look for information; timing; cost-effective channels.推廣：哪些客群要經營、顧客在哪找資訊、時機、有效率的管道。
    • S4a: the 4Ps are “the controllable variables the firm blends to produce the response it wants in the target market” (People? added). S5a: “the marketing mix must support the strategy” – action, not just talk.S4a：4P 是「公司組合起來、在目標市場產生想要反應的可控變數」（另加 People?）。S5a：「行銷組合必須支持策略」——要做到，不只是說。

    How to use it in an answer: first state the target and position (STP), then give one line per P that supports that position. Check consistency: a premium position with a discount price is confused.答題用法：先講目標與定位（STP），再每個 P 寫一行支持它。檢查一致性：高級定位配折扣價就是定位混亂。

    Marketing funnelS1

    Customers move from awareness to interest, trial and repeat purchase; each step can fail, so each needs its own objective and measure.顧客從知道、有興趣、試用到回購；每一步都可能斷掉，所以每一步要有自己的目標與指標。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： mentions views, sign-ups, first purchases, repeat visits or loyalty提到觀看、註冊、首次購買、回訪或忠誠 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Treating awareness, views or sign-ups as if they were sales把知名度、觀看或註冊當成銷售

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Without awareness, interest is difficult; awareness does not ensure interest; interest does not ensure trial; trial does not ensure repeat (S6 review).沒有知名度很難有興趣；有知名度不保證有興趣；有興趣不保證試用；試用不保證回購（S6 複習）。
    • Use it to diagnose where customers drop out, then pick the matching action and metric: awareness/consideration, conversion, repeat rate.用它診斷顧客在哪一關流失，再選對應的行動與指標：知名度／考慮、轉換、回購率。
    • S2 marketing metrics ask the same: what is our objective – awareness? interest? trial? reminder? repurchase? Views and likes can be faked.S2 行銷指標問的一樣：目標是知名度、興趣、試用、提醒還是回購？觀看與按讚可能是假的。

    Qualitative vs Quantitative researchS3

    Qualitative research is for understanding (why, how); quantitative research is for measuring (who, how often, how much).質性研究用來理解（為什麼、如何）；量化研究用來衡量（誰、多常、多少）。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks “which research method would you recommend for each…”問「每一項建議什麼研究方法」 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Choosing a survey “because the sample is large” without saying what information is needed說「用問卷因為樣本大」，卻沒說需要什麼資訊

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    The professor’s two questions老師的兩個問題

    • 1. What kind of information are you looking for?1. 你要找哪一種資訊？
    • 2. What sort of understanding? Who? How often? How much? → quantitative. How? Why? → qualitative.2. 哪一種理解？誰？多常？多少？→ 量化；如何？為什麼？→ 質性。

    Qualitative質性

    • About understanding; grounded in psychology目的是理解；以心理學為基礎
    • Usually focus groups with a moderator (1.5–2+ hours), or in-depth interviews; flexible discussion guide通常是焦點團體加主持人（1.5–2 小時以上）或深度訪談；討論大綱有彈性
    • For exploring and developing ideas, concepts, ads, packages; motivations; “why people do/don’t use”用來探索與發展點子、概念、廣告、包裝；了解動機、為什麼用或不用
    • Not for counting, replication or subgroup differences不適合計數、重複驗證或比較次群體

    Quantitative量化

    • About measuring; represents the population目的是衡量；能代表母體
    • Awareness and usage, brands used, how often, satisfaction, taste tests知名度與使用、用哪些品牌、多常買、滿意度、口味測試
    • Survey types: telephone, door-to-door, mall intercept, CLT, self-administered online or mobile問卷方式：電話、到府、商場攔訪、定點測試、線上或手機自填

    Dangers要注意的偏誤

    • Questions too direct give rational answers; people support their own behavior; courtesy bias (polite, positive)問題太直接只得到理性答案；人會替自己的行為辯護；客套偏誤
    • Correlation is not causation; statistics don’t explain why相關不等於因果；統計不會告訴你原因

    Research types: descriptive (who uses, how much), diagnostic (what’s going on), predictive (what would happen if…). Objectives come first; the method follows.研究類型：描述性（誰用、用多少）、診斷性（發生什麼事）、預測性（如果…會怎樣）。先定目標，方法跟著來。

    BCG growth–share matrix · Product life cycleS4a

    Classifies business units or products by market growth rate and relative market share into Stars, Question Marks, Cash Cows and Dogs.用市場成長率與相對市占，把事業單位或產品分成明星、問號、金牛、狗。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks about a company’s portfolio of products or businesses問到公司的產品或事業組合 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Applying it to countries as if it were exact; assuming Question Mark → Star is automatic把它當精確工具套到國家；以為問號一定會變明星

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Axes: market growth rate (high/low) × relative market share (high/low). It classifies SBUs (strategic business units).兩軸：市場成長率（高／低）× 相對市占（高／低）；分類的對象是 SBU（策略事業單位）。
    • Star = high growth, high share. Question mark = high growth, low share. Cash cow = low growth, high share. Dog = low growth, low share.明星＝高成長高市占；問號＝高成長低市占；金牛＝低成長高市占；狗＝低成長低市占。
    • Possible strategies: build share (invest), hold share, harvest, divest.可能策略：建立市占（投資）、維持、收割、撤資。
    • Each unit has its own life cycle (product life cycle).每個單位都有自己的生命週期。

    Slide example: Where would a hybrid car fit? Breeze? A home phone? Smart phones? EVs?講義例子：油電車在哪一格？Breeze？家用電話？智慧手機？電動車？

    Product life cycle: introduction → growth → maturity → decline. BCG positions are snapshots on two axes; the PLC describes how sales change over time. Don’t treat Question Mark → Star → Cash Cow → Dog as automatic.產品生命週期：導入 → 成長 → 成熟 → 衰退。BCG 是兩軸上的快照；PLC 描述銷售隨時間變化。不要把問號 → 明星 → 金牛 → 狗當成必然。

    SWOT analysisS4a

    Strengths and weaknesses are internal to the company; opportunities and threats are external, including competitors.優勢與劣勢在公司內部；機會與威脅在外部，包含競爭者。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks for a SWOT, or a strategic choice between options要求做 SWOT，或在幾個選項中做策略選擇 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Putting market trends under Strengths; listing a SWOT but never using it to decide把市場趨勢放進優勢；列了 SWOT 卻沒用它做決定

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • S – Strengths (internal, helpful): what we do well, our strongest assets (strong brand, loyal customers, unique tech).S 優勢（內部、有利）：我們做得好的、最強的資產（品牌、忠誠顧客、獨特技術）。
    • W – Weaknesses (internal, harmful): what holds us back (high turnover, debt, weak supply chain, low-performing lines).W 劣勢（內部、不利）：拖累我們的（流動率高、負債、供應鏈弱、表現差的產品線）。
    • O – Opportunities (external, helpful): trends in the market, segments we are not targeting, tariff cuts.O 機會（外部、有利）：市場趨勢、還沒鎖定的客群、關稅下降。
    • T – Threats (external, harmful): competitors and their share, new regulations, rising costs.T 威脅（外部、不利）：競爭者與其市占、新法規、成本上升。
    • The slide says SWOT covers “our own + competitors”. Test for placement: can the company control it? Yes → S/W. No → O/T.講義寫 SWOT 要看「自己＋競爭者」。分類測試：公司能控制嗎？能 → S/W；不能 → O/T。
    • A SWOT is evidence, not an answer: finish by matching strengths to opportunities and saying what you decide.SWOT 是證據不是答案：最後要把優勢對上機會，說出你的決定。

    Buyer decision process · Involvement · RiskS4b

    Consumers move through five stages; how many they really go through depends on involvement and perceived risk.消費者會經過五個階段；實際走幾步取決於涉入度與感知風險。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks how customers decide, or how to influence a purchase問顧客如何決定，或如何影響購買 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Reciting the five stages without applying them to the customer in the case只背出五個階段，沒有套到題幹的顧客

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Five stages: need recognition → information search → evaluation of alternatives → purchase decision → post-purchase behavior.五階段：需求確認 → 資訊搜尋 → 方案評估 → 購買決策 → 購後行為。
    • Involvement: linked to risk, importance and time used. Buying a car vs a Coke; facial cream vs floor cleaner. Low involvement → little search, choice by habit.涉入度：和風險、重要性、花的時間有關。買車 vs 買可樂；保養品 vs 地板清潔劑。低涉入 → 幾乎不搜尋，靠習慣。
    • Risk types: product performance risk, social risk, financial risk. How big? It depends on category and situation.風險類型：產品表現風險、社會風險、財務風險；大小取決於品類與情境。
    • Evoked set = all brands you know; consideration set = brands you would consider buying.喚起集合＝你知道的所有品牌；考慮名單＝你會考慮買的品牌。
    • Cognitive dissonance: anxiety or regret after a difficult, expensive or long-term decision; advertising can reassure buyers.認知失調：困難、昂貴或長期承諾的決定後的焦慮或後悔；廣告可以安撫。
    • Diffusion: innovators 2.5%, early adopters 13.5%, early majority 34%, late majority 34%, laggards 16%.創新擴散：創新者 2.5%、早期採用者 13.5%、早期大眾 34%、晚期大眾 34%、落後者 16%。

    Utilitarian vs Hedonic motivationS4b

    Utilitarian needs are practical and functional; hedonic needs are about pleasure, fun and experience. Both are often active at the same time.功能性需求講實用；享樂性需求講樂趣與體驗。兩者常同時存在。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks why customers buy, or what benefit a brand should stand for問顧客為什麼買，或品牌應主打什麼利益 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Treating a purchase as purely one or the other把購買當成只有其中一種

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Utilitarian: objective, functional product attributes or benefits. Hedonic / experiential: subjective pleasure, desire for fun. A car is functional but can also offer status, luxury and fun.功能性：客觀、功能性的屬性或利益。享樂／體驗性：主觀的愉悅、想要樂趣。車子實用，也代表地位、奢華與駕駛樂趣。
    • S4b “analyze the variables”: why do people drink coffee – canned, instant, fresh, at home or in a café? Functional benefits, emotional benefits, situation and taste all weigh in.S4b「分析變數」：人為什麼喝咖啡——罐裝、即溶、現煮、在家或咖啡店？功能利益、情感利益、情境、口味都有權重。
    • Bottled water: people pay for health, taste, fear of pollutants, snob appeal – the same product can be bought for different motives.瓶裝水：人們為健康、口味、怕污染、面子而買——同一產品可能因不同動機被買。
    • Blind taste tests: most people cannot tell brands apart without labels (about 8% can).盲測：多數人沒標籤分不出品牌（約 8% 嘗得出）。

    How to use it: say which motive drives the target in this occasion, then position on the motive rivals serve badly.答題用法：說出這個情境下目標客群的主要動機，再定位在對手做不好的那種動機上。

    STP · Targeting strategies · PositioningS5a

    Segment the market, choose the segments to target, then plan the position the product should hold in customers’ minds compared with competitors.區隔市場、選擇目標客群，再規劃產品相對競爭者在顧客心中的位置。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks about targeting, brand image, moving up- or down-market, or a sub-brand問目標客群、品牌形象、高檔化或低價化、子品牌 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Changing positioning abruptly; mixing two brands’ roles突然改定位；把兩個品牌的角色混在一起

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    Segmentation區隔

    • Bases: geographic, demographic, psychographic, family life cycle.依據：地理、人口統計、心理統計、家庭生命週期。
    • Effective segments are measurable (size, purchasing power), accessible (can be reached), substantial (large and profitable enough), actionable (can be served).有效區隔要可衡量、可接觸、夠大、可執行。

    Targeting選目標

    • Consider current and potential competitors, threat of substitutes, buyer and supplier power, and the company’s objectives and resources.考慮現有與潛在競爭者、替代品威脅、買方與供應商議價力、公司目標與資源。
    • Undifferentiated: one product for everyone (gum). Differentiated: separate offers for different segments – more sales but higher costs (Breeze, Omo, Sunsilk, Dove). Concentrated: a large share of one niche – risk of all eggs in one basket.無差異：一個產品給所有人。差異化：不同區隔不同產品，銷售多但成本高（Breeze、Omo）。集中：在一個利基拿高市占，風險集中。

    Positioning定位

    • A product’s position is where it sits in the consumer’s mind compared with competing products. Don’t leave it to chance.定位是產品相對對手在顧客心中的位置，不要放任自然形成。
    • Three steps: (1) identify possible competitive advantages; (2) select the right one(s) – one difference = USP; (3) communicate and deliver it – the marketing mix must support it.三步：(1) 找出可能的競爭優勢；(2) 選對的（一個差異＝USP）；(3) 傳達並做到——行銷組合要支持。
    • Differentiation types: product, services, personnel (better-trained people), image (symbols, colours, celebrities). Most advantages are temporary.差異化類型：產品、服務、人員（訓練更好的員工）、形象（符號、顏色、名人）。多數優勢是暫時的。
    • Errors: underpositioning (no clear idea), overpositioning (too narrow), confused positioning (mixed image).錯誤：定位不足、定位過窄、定位混亂。
    • Avoid abrupt changes: they confuse consumers, who may resist; a position should evolve gradually.避免突然改變：顧客會困惑、抗拒；定位應逐步演進。

    The professor’s caution: don’t believe in STP too much – anyone can buy anything, and most people are not 100% loyal.老師提醒：別太迷信 STP，任何人都可能買任何東西，多數人也不是 100% 忠誠。

    Demographic vs Psychographic segmentationS4b · S5a

    Demographics divide people by measurable traits (age, gender, income); psychographics divide them by interests, activities, opinions, values and lifestyle.人口統計用可測量的特徵（年齡、性別、所得）區分人；心理統計用興趣、活動、意見、價值觀與生活型態區分。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks how to segment or target customers, or why people of the same age choose differently問如何區隔或選目標客群，或為什麼同年齡的人選擇不同 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Segmenting only by age (“young people”) as if everyone that age wants the same thing只用年齡區隔（「年輕人」），好像同年齡的人都要一樣的東西

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Demographics: gender, age, marital and parental status, health, financial status. The most popular and easy to measure, but “age is often a poor predictor” (S5a).人口統計：性別、年齡、婚姻、子女、健康、財務。最常用也最好量，但「年齡常常預測力差」（S5a）。
    • Psychographics: interests, activities, opinions; values and attitudes; lifestyle; concerns; hobbies; spending habits; social class and personality. VALS groups people by values and lifestyles (achievers, experiencers, makers…).心理統計：興趣、活動、意見；價值觀與態度；生活型態；關心的事；嗜好；消費習慣；社會階層與個性。VALS 依價值觀與生活型態分群。
    • The professor: “I generally teach that we should focus on psychographic segmentation – reach as many people as possible using the best common denominators.” Digital targeting makes precise targeting easier.老師：「我通常教大家著重心理統計區隔——用最好的共同點觸及最多人。」數位廣告讓精準鎖定更容易。
    • S6 review: same age ≠ same motivation; same person ≠ same purchase every time. Demographics help for some goods (cat food, baby products) but leave much unexplained elsewhere.S6 複習：同年齡 ≠ 同動機；同一個人也不會每次買一樣的。某些商品（貓食、嬰兒用品）人口統計有用，其他常常解釋不了。
    • Check any segment with S5a’s four tests: measurable, accessible, substantial, actionable.任何區隔都用 S5a 四個條件檢查：可衡量、可接觸、夠大、可執行。

    A two-axis diagram of how consumers perceive brands; each axis is a trait customers care about.用兩個軸呈現顧客對品牌的認知；每個軸是顧客在意的一個特性。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks you to “plot” or “map” competitors要求把競爭者「畫」在圖上 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Two axes that measure the same thing (price and quality), so all points sit on one line兩軸量同一件事（價格與品質），點排成一條線

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    • Test your axes: if all points fall on one diagonal, the axes say the same thing – change one.檢查軸：如果所有點都在同一條斜線上，兩軸在講同一件事，換掉一個。

    Direct vs Indirect competitionS5b

    Direct competitors offer the same category to the same target; indirect competitors offer a different product that can satisfy the same need.直接競爭者在同品類、同客群；間接競爭者提供不同產品，但能滿足同一個需要。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks for direct or indirect competitors要你列直接或間接對手 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Listing a same-category rival as “indirect”把同品類對手寫成間接

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Direct: same category and market, usually the same target (Coca-Cola vs Pepsi).直接：同品類、同市場，通常同客群（可口可樂 vs 百事）。
    • Indirect: different products that can be substituted to fulfil the same need (Coca-Cola vs Lipton tea). Even time is contested: TV, internet, games compete for entertainment.間接：不同產品但可替代、滿足同一需要（可口可樂 vs 立頓茶）。連時間也在競爭：電視、網路、遊戲都搶娛樂時間。
    • Practical test (S6): if a customer could choose it instead of yours for that occasion, it is a competitor. Name the category and occasion first.實用判準（S6）：顧客在那個情境可以改買它，它就是競爭者。先講清楚品類與情境。
    • Market types: pure monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition, pure competition. Entry barriers (hard to start) and exit barriers (hard to leave).市場類型：獨占、寡占、壟斷性競爭、完全競爭。進入障礙與退出障礙。
    • Customer value analysis: value = benefits − costs; consumers may not be that rational.顧客價值分析：價值＝利益 − 成本；顧客不一定那麼理性。
    • Competitive strategies (S5b): offense vs defense; market leader vs follower (technology); competitive intelligence – analyse competitors and trends, but don’t spend so much that you become myopic.競爭策略（S5b）：攻擊 vs 防守；領導者 vs 跟隨者；競爭情報——分析對手與趨勢，但別花太多變成近視。
    • Brand stretching: when a brand extends into a new category, ask whether the new products are direct or indirect competitors of existing ones.品牌延伸：品牌跨入新品類時，要問新產品和現有產品是直接還是間接競爭。

    Relationship marketing · CRM · Lifetime valueS2

    Focus on long-lasting customer relationships rather than single transactions, because repeat customers bring long-term revenue.重視長期顧客關係而非單次交易，因為回頭客帶來長期收入。

    Six marketing management philosophiesS1

    Six ways a company can think about marketing, from production-focused to societal and strategic marketing.公司看待行銷的六種思維，從重生產到社會行銷與策略行銷。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks about CSR, ethics, health, or “sell what customers want vs what is good for them”問 CSR、倫理、健康，或「賣顧客想要的 vs 對他們好的」 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Saying “CSR is good” without naming the societal marketing concept只說「CSR 很好」，沒有點名 societal marketing concept

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Production concept: customers want products that are available and affordable → focus on production and distribution efficiency (Ford Model T, “any color as long as it’s black”).生產觀念：顧客要買得到、買得起 → 專注生產與配銷效率（福特 T 型車）。
    • Product concept: customers want the most quality and features → keep improving the product. Risk: marketing myopia and constant me-too copying.產品觀念：顧客要最好品質與功能 → 不斷改良產品。風險：行銷近視、不斷被模仿。
    • Selling concept: people won’t buy enough unless pushed by selling and promotion; common for unsought goods.銷售觀念：不強力推銷顧客就不會買夠；常見於非尋求品。
    • Marketing concept: understand the needs and wants of target markets and satisfy them better than competitors. Many firms claim it but don’t commit.行銷觀念：了解目標市場的需要與想要，做得比對手好。很多公司說有，其實沒做到。
    • Societal marketing concept: questions whether the marketing concept is enough, because it may focus on short-term gains despite long-term effects on people and society.社會行銷觀念：質疑只滿足需要是否足夠，因為可能只顧短期利益、忽略對人與社會的長期影響。
    • Strategic marketing concept: when offers are similar in quality, price and service, the firm with the better profit margin wins; logistics takes cost out of the system (Coke vs Pepsi).策略行銷觀念：品質價格服務都差不多時，毛利較好的贏；用物流降低成本（可口可樂 vs 百事）。

    Linked ideas in S1: CSR, sustainability, “consumption does have limits”, the UN SDGs. Question to reflect on: is marketing basically anti-sustainability?S1 相關觀念：CSR、永續、「消費有極限」、聯合國 SDGs。老師的反思題：行銷本質上是反永續的嗎？

    Deceptive marketing: downsizingS5b

    Instead of raising prices, companies shrink the product or pack, which makes prices hard to compare and is easy to miss.不漲價而是縮小產品或包裝，讓人很難比價、也不容易發現。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： mentions smaller packs, shrinking sizes or hidden price rises提到包裝變小、份量縮水或隱藏漲價 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Mixing up the two percentages (less product vs higher unit price)把兩個百分比搞混（份量減少 vs 單價上升）

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • The slides put downsizing under deceptive marketing: many sizes make it very difficult to compare prices; few people check the grams or remember last time’s size.講義把縮量放在欺騙性行銷：規格很多很難比價；很少人看克數或記得上次多少。
    • Examples: cans 355 → 330 → 320 ml; Nestlé 830 → 800 ml; 2 cans (660 ml) vs 1 big bottle (640 ml).例子：罐裝 355 → 330 → 320 ml；Nestlé 830 → 800 ml；兩罐 660 ml vs 一大瓶 640 ml。
    • Two different calculations: product reduction = (old − new) ÷ old; unit-price rise = old ÷ new − 1. For 330 → 320 ml: 3.0% less drink, 3.1% higher price per ml.兩種算法：份量減少＝(舊−新)÷舊；單價上升＝舊÷新−1。330 → 320 ml：少 3.0%，每 ml 貴 3.1%。
    • Link to S1: the societal marketing concept and CSR ask whether short-term gains are worth long-term harm to trust.連到 S1：社會行銷觀念與 CSR 問的是短期利益是否值得犧牲長期信任。

    Marketing myopia · USP · Me-too and brandingS1

    Focusing on the product instead of the benefit customers want is marketing myopia; most features can be copied, so a difference must be a benefit people value, and branding matters.只看產品而忘了顧客要的利益就是行銷近視；多數功能都會被抄，所以差異要是顧客重視的利益，品牌因此重要。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks how to differentiate or what a brand should stand for問如何差異化或品牌應代表什麼 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： A “unique” feature customers do not care about (different just to be different)顧客不在乎的「獨特」功能（為不同而不同）

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Marketing myopia: focusing on the product rather than the benefit being satisfied; failing to see challenges from other products or changes in the environment.行銷近視：只看產品而非被滿足的利益；看不到其他產品或環境變化帶來的挑戰。
    • USP: “USP, but is it a benefit people want or need? Different just to be different…”USP：「是獨特賣點，但那是顧客想要或需要的利益嗎？還是為不同而不同？」
    • Me-too: who made the first 3- or 5-megapixel camera phone? Nobody remembers – everyone copied. “Everything can usually be copied”, which moves us to the importance of branding; “it very often is not about superiority”.Me-too：誰做了第一支 300 萬畫素手機？沒人記得，大家都抄。「幾乎什麼都能被抄」，所以品牌很重要；「常常不是比誰更好」。
    • S6 example: beer made with “iceberg water” – a distinctive ingredient with no clear benefit customers would pay for.S6 例子：「冰山水」啤酒——成分很特別，但顧客看不到值得多付錢的利益。

    Competitive strategies: new users, new uses, more usageS5b

    Three ways to increase sales: find new users, find new uses for the product, or get existing users to use more.三種增加銷售的方式：找新使用者、找新用途、讓現有使用者用更多。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks for ideas to increase the customer base and sales要你提增加顧客與銷售的點子 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Giving two ideas that use the same source of growth兩個點子用同一種成長來源

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • New users – how realistic? Easier in under-penetrated categories.新使用者——實際嗎？在滲透率低的品類較容易。
    • New uses – baking soda sales rose hugely as new uses were found; calcium from antacids; Rogaine; Viagra.新用途——小蘇打找到新用途後銷量大增；制酸劑補鈣；Rogaine；Viagra。
    • More usage – bigger packs, more occasions (champagne trying to move beyond special occasions).更多用量——大包裝、更多場合（香檳想走出特殊場合）。
    • Ethics: planned obsolescence (Gillette) and pushing overconsumption are questionable.倫理：計畫性汰舊（Gillette）與鼓勵過度消費值得質疑。

    Same idea in Ansoff (S4a): market penetration includes increasing frequency, quantity and new applications.Ansoff（S4a）裡的同一概念：市場滲透包含增加頻率、用量與新用途。

    Four growth routes from existing or new products in existing or new markets; risk rises as you move away from what you already know.用「現有／新產品 × 現有／新市場」分出四種成長路線；離熟悉的越遠，風險越高。

    Needs, Wants & DemandsS1

    A need is a felt deprivation; a want is the form a need takes, shaped by culture and personality; a demand is a want backed by buying power.需要是感到缺乏；想要是需要被文化與個性塑造後的形式；需求是有購買力支撐的想要。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： compares markets, incomes or asks whether people will actually buy比較市場、所得，或問顧客會不會真的買 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Treating a large population as large demand把人口多當成需求大

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Need: a state of felt deprivation. Basic (food, clothing, shelter, safety), social (belonging, affection), individual (knowledge, self-expression). Linked to Maslow.需要：感到缺乏的狀態。基本（食衣住、安全）、社會（歸屬、情感）、個人（知識、自我表現）；可連到 Maslow。
    • Want: the form a need takes as shaped by culture and individual personality. Need: hunger → want: noodles, sushi, steak.想要：需要被文化與個人個性塑造後的形式。需要：餓 → 想要：麵、壽司、牛排。
    • Demand: wants backed by buying power (age and income of the population). Wants are unlimited, money is limited, so consumers look for the best value for their money.需求：有購買力支撐的想要（看人口年齡與所得）。想要無限、錢有限，所以顧客會找最划算的。

    How to use it in an answer: “Population growth only matters if incomes rise enough to turn wants into demand.”答題用法：「人口成長要搭配所得上升，想要才會變成需求。」

    Slide example: Basil pork rice for the whole class: not everyone is satisfied (utility). Even satisfied customers may not buy again; beware of believing in loyalty.講義例子：請全班吃同一道打拋豬飯，不是每個人都滿意（效用）；滿意也不保證回購，別迷信忠誠。

    Observation · ExperimentsS3

    Observation records what people actually do; experiments change one variable to see its effect.觀察記錄人們實際做了什麼；實驗改變一個變數看效果。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks how customers actually behave, or whether an action caused a result問顧客實際怎麼做，或某行動是否造成結果 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Using a survey to find out what people actually do用問卷去了解人們「實際」做什麼

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Observation avoids many survey biases: it records what people did, not what they say. But it only shows the surface, not motives, attitudes or feelings; it can be slow and costly.觀察避開問卷偏誤：記錄做了什麼而非說了什麼；但只看到表面，看不到動機與感受，且耗時。
    • Mystery shopper: researchers pose as customers to evaluate prices, displays, service; can be used on competitors too.神秘客：假扮顧客評估價格、陳列、服務；也可用在競爭者。
    • Experiment: O = observation (measure), X = treatment (change). Compare X O with O X O. Example: week-3 sales (O) → install a display (X) → week-4 sales (O). Watch for spurious causes.實驗：O = 觀察（量測）、X = 處理（改變）。比較 X O 與 O X O。例：第 3 週銷售 → 放陳列架 → 第 4 週銷售。小心其他干擾因素。
    • Lab vs field: a lab gives control but is artificial; field experiments are realistic but less controlled.實驗室 vs 實地：實驗室好控制但不真實；實地真實但難控制。
    • Other tools from S3: conjoint analysis (trade-offs between attributes), cluster analysis (profiling groups).S3 其他工具：聯合分析（屬性取捨）、集群分析（描繪族群）。

    Use it when the question…題目出現什麼就用： asks to profile or segment customers, or how they trade off price and features要描繪或區隔顧客，或問價格與功能的取捨 · Common mistake:常見錯誤： Naming a method before stating what information is needed還沒說需要什麼資訊就先選方法

    Managing People in Organizations · Quick sheet

    Definitions as keywords

    One card per framework from S1–S6: a few keywords and one plain sentence you can write in the exam.

    Practice mode blurs the keywords and sentences. Look at the title, say them yourself, then click to check.

    How to use these cards

    You do not need the slide definition word for word. The marker wants to see that you know what the framework means and can use it on the case. So for each one, remember two or three keywords, then join them into one plain sentence in your own English. That sentence is your definition.

    定義不用背到一字不差。每個框架記 2–3 個關鍵字，考試時用自己的簡單英文串成一句就是定義。練法：打開 Practice mode，看標題自己說出關鍵字和那句話，再點開對答案。每天 5 張就好。

    Turn keywords into a definition[Framework] says that [keyword 1] and [keyword 2].例：Herzberg says that hygiene factors only prevent dissatisfaction, while motivators create motivation.
    Then go straight to the caseIn the case, [fact]. This shows [gap] because [reason].定義只要一句，馬上接個案事實。分數在後面這句。

    S1 · Personality

    Personality explains preferences. Use it for “how does this person like to work” and “how should I talk to them”.

    MBTI: E / I

    where energy comes fromthink out loud vs think first

    E/I describes where a person gets energy: extraverts work ideas out by talking, introverts prefer to think first on their own.

    外向／內向：能量從哪來、邊講邊想還是想好再講。

    MBTI: S / N

    facts and detailsbig picture and possibilities

    S/N describes how a person takes in information: sensing focuses on concrete facts, intuition on patterns and future possibilities.

    實感／直覺：看細節事實，還是看整體與可能性。

    MBTI: T / F

    logic and criteriavalues and impact on people

    T/F describes how a person decides: thinking uses logic and criteria, feeling weighs values and the effect on people.

    思考／情感：用邏輯判斷，還是看對人的影響。

    MBTI: J / P

    planned and settledflexible and open

    J/P describes how a person deals with the outside world: judging prefers plans and closure, perceiving prefers to keep options open.

    判斷／感知：喜歡事情定下來，還是保持彈性。

    Strengths used too much

    every preference is usefuloverused → weakness

    Each preference is a strength, but used too much it becomes a weakness, for example attention to detail turning into micromanagement.

    優點用過頭就變缺點。

    Preference ≠ ability

    what you preferwhat you can learn to do

    A preference is what feels natural, not a limit. An N-type can still learn to plan details.

    偏好不等於能力。

    People Management Performance Model

    leader · people · culture · processperformance + satisfactioninside a context

    Performance and satisfaction come from the leader, the people, the work culture and the work process together, inside the national, industry and organizational context.

    績效不只看個人人格，還有主管、團隊、文化、流程。

    Three managerial skills

    technicalconceptualhuman

    Managers need technical skill to do the work, conceptual skill to see the whole picture, and human skill to work with people. Personality links mainly to human skill.

    技術、概念、人際三種能力。

    S2 · Values and attitudes

    Use these when the case is about what people care about, whether they fit the company, or how they feel about the job.

    Terminal vs instrumental values

    end goalway of behaving

    Terminal values are the end states a person wants, such as freedom or security. Instrumental values are the ways of behaving that get them there, such as honesty or ambition.

    終極價值是目的，工具性價值是做法。

    Person–organization fit

    personal valuescompany valuesmatch → satisfaction

    When a person’s values match the organization’s, they are more satisfied and more likely to stay. People pick companies that fit them, or leave.

    人跟組織的價值觀合不合。

    Stated vs practiced values

    what the company sayswhat it rewards and does

    A stated value only counts if leaders model it, rewards and policies support it, and it is communicated again and again. When practice contradicts it, there is a stated–practice gap.

    說一套做一套：看主管行為、獎勵制度、反覆溝通。

    Attitude: ABC

    cognitive = beliefaffective = feelingbehavioral = action / intention

    An attitude has three parts: what a person believes (cognitive), how they feel (affective) and what they do or intend to do (behavioral).

    態度三要素：想法、感受、行為。

    Job satisfaction

    positive feeling about the job

    Job satisfaction is an attitude of fulfillment towards one’s job. It does not prove the person will stay or perform well.

    對工作感到滿足；不代表一定留下或表現好。

    Job involvement

    identify with the job

    Job involvement is how strongly a person identifies with their job and sees it as part of who they are.

    把工作當成自我的一部分的程度。

    Organizational commitment

    accept goals and valueswork hard for itwant to stay

    Organizational commitment means accepting the organization’s goals and values, being willing to work hard for it, and wanting to remain a member.

    認同組織目標價值、願意努力、想留下。

    OCB

    voluntaryextra-roleno direct reward

    Organizational citizenship behavior is voluntary extra work that helps the organization and is not rewarded directly, such as helping a new colleague.

    組織公民行為：自願、職責外、沒有直接獎勵。

    Type A / Type B

    A: impatient, competitive, time-consciousB: relaxed, patient

    Type A people are impatient, competitive and time-conscious; Type B people are more relaxed. Type A suits target-driven work but risks burnout and quality problems.

    A 型急躁好勝重時效；B 型較放鬆。沒有誰一定比較好。

    Changing attitudes

    their attitudeyou (trust, credibility)your strategy

    Whether you can change someone’s attitude depends on how strongly they hold it, how much they trust you, and the strategy you use.

    能不能改變別人態度：看他態度多強、多信任你、你的方法。

    Culture Development Framework

    role modelpromote from withincommunicate valuesrecruit by valuesavoid layoffs

    Ketprapakorn and Kantabutra suggest building culture through leaders as role models, promotion from within, constant value communication, hiring for values and avoiding layoffs.

    建立文化的五個做法。

    S3 · Motivation

    Use these for “why are people not motivated” questions. Herzberg is the one for “why does money not work”.

    Intrinsic vs extrinsic

    from the work itselffrom outside rewards

    Intrinsic motivation comes from the work itself, like enjoying a challenge. Extrinsic motivation comes from outside rewards, like pay or a bonus.

    內在動機來自工作本身，外在動機來自獎勵。

    Maslow

    physiologicalsafetysocialesteemself-actualization

    Maslow says people have five levels of needs, from physiological and safety up to social, esteem and self-actualization. I check which levels the workplace meets and which it does not.

    五層需求，一層一層檢查有沒有被滿足。

    ERG

    existencerelatednessgrowthfrustration → regression

    ERG groups needs into existence, relatedness and growth, which can matter at the same time. If growth is blocked, people may fall back and ask more for lower needs, such as money.

    三類需求可同時存在；成長受挫會退回要求較低層需求。

    Herzberg

    hygiene → prevents dissatisfactionmotivators → create motivation

    Herzberg says hygiene factors such as pay, policy and supervision only prevent dissatisfaction, while motivators such as achievement, recognition, responsibility and growth create motivation.

    保健因子做好只是不扣分；激勵因子才會讓人想多做。薪水是保健因子。

    Theory X / Y

    X: people avoid work, need controlY: people take responsibilityself-fulfilling

    Theory X assumes people dislike work and need control; Theory Y assumes they can direct themselves and take responsibility. A manager’s assumption tends to create the behavior it expects.

    主管對人的假設；X 會自我實現：管得越死，員工越被動。

    Goal setting / MBO

    specifictime periodparticipationfeedback

    Good goals are specific, have a time period, are set with the employee and come with regular feedback. Goals should cascade from the top so each level supports the one above.

    MBO 四要素：具體、有期限、共同設定、回饋；目標要一層層往下對齊。

    Issue vs symptom

    symptom = what you seeissue = gap explained by a framework

    A symptom is what you see, such as low morale. An issue is the gap between the framework and reality that explains it.

    症狀是看到的現象；問題是用框架解釋出來的落差。

    S4 · Job design, responses and rewards

    Use these when the question is about the job itself, how people react, or what a reward rule encourages.

    Job Characteristics Model

    skill varietytask identitytask significanceautonomyfeedback

    The JCM says a job motivates when it uses different skills, lets people finish a whole piece of work, matters to others, gives freedom and provides feedback. These create meaning, responsibility and knowledge of results.

    五個構面 → 三種心理狀態（有意義、有責任感、知道結果）。

    Growth-need strength

    who wants growth

    People with high growth needs react most strongly to a richer job; others may prefer stable routines.

    成長需求高的人對工作豐富化反應最大。

    EVLN

    ExitVoiceLoyaltyNeglectactive/passive × constructive/destructive

    EVLN shows four responses to dissatisfaction: leaving (exit), speaking up (voice), waiting for things to improve (loyalty) and doing less (neglect).

    不滿的四種反應。投影片把遲到、缺勤放在 Exit。

    Reinforcement

    positive: add good → morenegative: remove bad → morepunishment: add bad → lessextinction: reward stops → fades

    Reinforcement theory says behavior that is rewarded is repeated. Positive reinforcement adds something good, negative reinforcement removes something unpleasant, punishment adds something unpleasant, and extinction stops the reward.

    給好東西、拿走討厭的東西都是讓行為變多；懲罰讓行為變少。

    Reinforcement schedules

    time → intervaloutput → ratiofixed or variable

    First ask if the reward depends on time (interval) or output (ratio), then whether it is fixed or variable. Piece rate is fixed ratio; commission is variable ratio; unannounced inspections are variable interval.

    先看看時間還是看件數，再看固定還是不固定。

    Six elements of recognition

    management supportstandardsmeasurementmeaningful consequencesfair applicationevaluation

    A recognition system works only with management support, clear standards, measurement, consequences people value, fair and regular use, and regular review of the system.

    KPI 只是六個要素之一。

    S5 · EQ

    Use these for questions about how a leader behaves under pressure, or for a coaching plan.

    EQ

    recognize emotionsmanage self and relationshipscan be developed

    Emotional intelligence is the ability to recognize emotions in yourself and others and to manage yourself and your relationships. It comes from nature and nurture, so it can be developed.

    辨識自己和別人的情緒，管理自己和關係；可以培養。

    1 · Self-awareness and self-control

    know your feelings and triggerschoose the response

    Self-awareness is knowing your emotions, triggers and values; self-control is not letting those emotions drive bad behavior.

    知道自己的情緒，並控制反應。

    2 · Empathy

    see it from their sidestill keep the standard

    Empathy is understanding how others see and feel a situation. It does not mean dropping the standard.

    理解對方的角度，但不代表放棄標準。

    3 · Social expertise

    handle conflictsupportbuild relationships

    Social expertise is handling interactions well: dealing with conflict, giving support and building relationships.

    處理衝突、互動、建立關係。

    4 · Personal influence

    inspirewilling support, not just orders

    Personal influence is getting people to follow willingly through words and actions, not only through authority.

    讓人心甘情願跟隨，不是只靠職權。

    5 · Mastery of vision

    purpose and valuesshared direction

    Mastery of vision is having a clear purpose and values and making sure people understand the shared direction. A number target alone is not a vision.

    有清楚的方向，且大家都懂；數字目標不等於願景。

    Developing EQ

    coaching and feedbackhonest assessmentpersonal planmeditation

    EQ can be developed through coaching and feedback, honest assessment from trusted people, a personal plan with work as the practice ground, and meditation.

    教練與回饋、誠實評估、個人計畫、冥想。

    S6 · Cross-cultural management

    Use these when people from different countries misread each other. Country scores are averages, so always say you are checking real behavior.

    Culture

    collective mental programminglearned, sharedhard to change

    Hofstede describes culture as the collective mental programming of a group, learned through shared experience, which is why it is hard to change.

    群體共同學來的思考預設，所以難改。

    High vs low context

    high: meaning in situation, tonelow: meaning in the words

    In high-context communication, meaning sits in the situation and relationship, like a Thai smile. In low-context communication, it is stated directly in words. Neither is better.

    高情境：意思在情境裡；低情境：意思在字面上。

    Power distance

    acceptance of unequal power

    Power distance is how far people accept unequal power as normal. Thailand 64, Switzerland 34, China 80, Russia 93.

    接受權力差距的程度。

    Individualism / collectivism

    I vs we

    Individualism is the focus on personal goals and independence; collectivism is the focus on the group. Thailand 19, Switzerland 79.

    我 vs 我們。

    Uncertainty avoidance

    preference for structure and rules

    Uncertainty avoidance is how much people prefer structured, predictable situations. It is about rules, not about authority.

    偏好規則、可預測；跟權力距離是兩回事。

    Masculinity / femininity

    achievement, money, assertivenesscare, harmony, quality of life

    Masculinity is a cultural focus on achievement and competition; femininity is a focus on care and quality of life. Thailand 34, Switzerland 70. It says nothing about the sex of employees.

    成就導向 vs 關懷導向，不是性別。

    Long-term orientation

    long-run vs short-run view

    Long-term orientation is how much a culture focuses on the future and persistence rather than quick results. Thailand 67.

    長期 vs 短期觀點。另有第六個 Indulgence（放縱 vs 克制）。

    Jai Yen · Jai Ron · Sanuk

    cool heart: calm, face, harmonyhot heart: direct, resultsenjoyment at work

    Jai Yen means staying calm and protecting face and harmony; Jai Ron is a direct, results-first style; Sanuk is enjoyment and relationships at work.

    冷靜留面子／直接重結果／工作要有樂趣。

    Personality × culture

    preference can strengthen or weaken a tendencydifferent constructs

    Personality can make a cultural tendency stronger or weaker, but they are different things: introversion is not individualism, and judging is not masculinity.

    I 不等於個人主義，J 不等於陽剛。

    Thailand’s five scores in the lecturer’s chart, in the order PD, IDV, MAS, UA, LTO: 64 / 19 / 34 / 64 / 67.

    bring in a factIn the case, ___.
    explain the gapUnder this framework, this shows ___ because ___.
    make an assumptionAssuming this is his usual habit, ___.
    say the evidence is not enoughThe case does not tell us ___, so I cannot conclude ___.

    say how to checkTo check, I would look at ___ after ___ weeks.

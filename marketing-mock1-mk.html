    MK chases younger diners as its loyal customers grow older

    MK Restaurant Group is Thailand’s largest suki (hot pot) chain, with more than 400 MK restaurants and a few branches abroad, including Vietnam and Japan. MK is known for consistent food, friendly staff and family meals, and its core customers are families and older diners.

    Younger diners increasingly choose cheaper all-you-can-eat buffets such as Suki Teenoi, or grill restaurants such as Bar B Q Plaza. MK runs its own delivery service but also sells through delivery apps that charge commission. Its large membership program shows that many young members join for a first-visit discount and never return, while older members visit less often each year.

    Management is considering more branches overseas and new ways to win younger customers.

    Practice case written from public information; figures are approximate and treated as case facts.練習用個案，依公開資訊改寫；數字為約略值，練習時視為題幹事實。

    Key facts to use as evidence可當證據的題幹重點

    • Largest hot pot chain, 400+ branches; a few abroad (Vietnam, Japan).泰國最大火鍋連鎖，400 多家；海外少量（越南、日本）。
    • Strengths: consistent food, friendly service, family dining; core = families and older diners.強項：品質一致、服務親切、家庭聚餐；核心＝家庭與年長者。
    • Young diners go to Suki Teenoi (cheap buffet) and Bar B Q Plaza (grill).年輕人改去 Suki Teenoi（平價吃到飽）與 Bar B Q Plaza（燒烤）。
    • Own delivery plus commission-charging delivery apps.自營外送＋收抽成的外送平台。
    • Young members take a first-visit discount and never return; older members visit less each year.年輕會員拿首次折扣就不再來；年長會員來店逐年減少。

    5Cs: Company, Customers, Competitors, Collaborators, ContextS1 · S5a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： The professor’s way to understand a situation before choosing: what the company has, who the customers are and what they want, who else competes, who helps deliver, and what is happening around them.老師分析現況的方法，在做選擇之前先看：公司有什麼、顧客是誰要什麼、還有誰在競爭、誰幫忙交付、周邊環境發生什麼。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： Compare Thailand and the chosen country across the 5Cs. The case describes Thailand in detail but says almost nothing about other countries, so the Thailand column carries the evidence and the other column shows what MK already has there and what must be checked.用 5C 比較泰國與選定國家。題幹把泰國寫得很詳細，其他國家幾乎沒寫，所以泰國那欄是證據，另一欄寫 MK 在那裡已經有什麼、還要查證什麼。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Company: strengths, resources, existing stores or products, capabilities.公司：優勢、資源、現有據點或產品、能力。
    • Customers: who they are and what they want – S1’s market size, age and income sit here; needs, wants and demand (wants backed by buying power).顧客：是誰、想要什麼——S1 的市場規模、年齡、所得放這裡；需要、想要、需求（有購買力的想要）。
    • Competitors: direct (same category) and indirect (other ways to meet the same need).競爭者：直接（同品類）與間接（滿足同一需要的其他方式）。
    • Collaborators: suppliers, distributors, retailers, partners, parent group businesses.合作者：供應商、通路、零售商、夥伴、母集團事業。
    • Context: economy and growth, regulation and politics, culture and religion, technology (the macro-environment of S4a).情境：經濟與成長、法規與政治、文化與宗教、科技（即 S4a 的總體環境）。

    5Cs vs SWOT: the same facts in two layouts. Company → Strengths and Weaknesses; Customers, Competitors, Collaborators and Context → Opportunities and Threats. Use SWOT when the question asks for it; otherwise a 5C contrast table is clearer for comparing two markets.5C 與 SWOT：同一批事實的兩種排法。公司 → 優勢與劣勢；顧客、競爭者、合作者、情境 → 機會與威脅。題目要 SWOT 就用 SWOT；比較兩個市場時，5C 對比表比較清楚。

    Product–market growth grid (often called Ansoff)S4aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Four growth routes from existing or new products in existing or new markets; risk rises as you move away from what you already know.用「現有／新產品 × 現有／新市場」分出四種成長路線；離熟悉的越遠，風險越高。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： More Thai branches = market penetration; another country = market development. Expanding where MK already has branches is the lower-risk version of market development.泰國再開店＝市場滲透；去另一國＝市場開發。在已有分店的國家擴張，是風險較低的市場開發。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Market penetration (existing product, existing market): increase market share; increase product usage – frequency of use, quantity used, new applications.市場滲透（現有產品、現有市場）：提高市占；增加使用——使用頻率、使用量、新用途。
    • Product development (new product, existing market): product improvement, product-line extensions, new products for the existing market.產品開發（新產品、現有市場）：產品改良、產品線延伸、為現有市場推新產品。
    • Market development (existing product, new market): geographic expansion, targeting a new segment.市場開發（現有產品、新市場）：地理擴張、鎖定新客群。
    • Diversification (new product, new market): forward/backward/horizontal integration; related (concentric) or unrelated (conglomerate).多角化（新產品、新市場）：前向／後向／水平整合；相關（同心）或不相關（集團式）。

    How to use it: name each option with a cell first (“opening more Thai stores is market penetration; Vietnam is market development”), then argue why the extra risk is or is not worth it.答題用法：先用格子替選項命名（「泰國再開店是市場滲透；越南是市場開發」），再論證多出來的風險值不值得。

    Note: the S4a slide shows this grid but does not name it; “Ansoff” is the common textbook name.注意：S4a 投影片有這張表但沒有命名；「Ansoff」是教科書常用名稱。

    I recommend MK prioritize Vietnam and expand there step by step. The case shows that growth in Thailand is becoming difficult: MK’s core customers are families and older diners who visit less each year, while young Thai diners choose cheaper buffets and grills and do not return after their first discount. MK therefore needs a new source of growth. First, Vietnam is the only ASEAN country in the case where MK already has branches, so it is not starting from zero.     ② Which framework (from the lecture slides)② 用哪個框架（老師講義）

    Demographic vs Psychographic segmentationS4b · S5a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： Demographics divide people by measurable traits (age, gender, income); psychographics divide them by interests, activities, opinions, values and lifestyle.人口統計用可測量的特徵（年齡、性別、所得）區分人；心理統計用興趣、活動、意見、價值觀與生活型態區分。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： The first half of the question is exactly this choice. The professor teaches psychographic segmentation and warns that age is often a poor predictor.前半題就是這個選擇。老師主張心理統計區隔，並提醒年齡常常預測力差。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Demographics: gender, age, marital and parental status, health, financial status. The most popular and easy to measure, but “age is often a poor predictor” (S5a).人口統計：性別、年齡、婚姻、子女、健康、財務。最常用也最好量，但「年齡常常預測力差」（S5a）。
    • Psychographics: interests, activities, opinions; values and attitudes; lifestyle; concerns; hobbies; spending habits; social class and personality. VALS groups people by values and lifestyles (achievers, experiencers, makers…).心理統計：興趣、活動、意見；價值觀與態度；生活型態；關心的事；嗜好；消費習慣；社會階層與個性。VALS 依價值觀與生活型態分群。

    STP · Targeting strategies · PositioningS5aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Segment the market, choose the segments to target, then plan the position the product should hold in customers’ minds compared with competitors.區隔市場、選擇目標客群，再規劃產品相對競爭者在顧客心中的位置。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    Segmentation區隔

    • Bases: geographic, demographic, psychographic, family life cycle.依據：地理、人口統計、心理統計、家庭生命週期。
    • Effective segments are measurable (size, purchasing power), accessible (can be reached), substantial (large and profitable enough), actionable (can be served).有效區隔要可衡量、可接觸、夠大、可執行。

    Targeting選目標

    • Consider current and potential competitors, threat of substitutes, buyer and supplier power, and the company’s objectives and resources.考慮現有與潛在競爭者、替代品威脅、買方與供應商議價力、公司目標與資源。
    • Undifferentiated: one product for everyone (gum). Differentiated: separate offers for different segments – more sales but higher costs (Breeze, Omo, Sunsilk, Dove). Concentrated: a large share of one niche – risk of all eggs in one basket.無差異：一個產品給所有人。差異化：不同區隔不同產品，銷售多但成本高（Breeze、Omo）。集中：在一個利基拿高市占，風險集中。

    Positioning定位

    • A product’s position is where it sits in the consumer’s mind compared with competing products. Don’t leave it to chance.定位是產品相對對手在顧客心中的位置，不要放任自然形成。
    • Three steps: (1) identify possible competitive advantages; (2) select the right one(s) – one difference = USP; (3) communicate and deliver it – the marketing mix must support it.三步：(1) 找出可能的競爭優勢；(2) 選對的（一個差異＝USP）；(3) 傳達並做到——行銷組合要支持。
    • Differentiation types: product, services, personnel (better-trained people), image (symbols, colours, celebrities). Most advantages are temporary.差異化類型：產品、服務、人員（訓練更好的員工）、形象（符號、顏色、名人）。多數優勢是暫時的。
    • Errors: underpositioning (no clear idea), overpositioning (too narrow), confused positioning (mixed image).錯誤：定位不足、定位過窄、定位混亂。
    • Avoid abrupt changes: they confuse consumers, who may resist; a position should evolve gradually.避免突然改變：顧客會困惑、抗拒；定位應逐步演進。

    The professor’s caution: don’t believe in STP too much – anyone can buy anything, and most people are not 100% loyal.老師提醒：別太迷信 STP，任何人都可能買任何東西，多數人也不是 100% 忠誠。

Since no customer is fully loyal, I would set realistic targets and track repeat rate, churn and revenue per member.

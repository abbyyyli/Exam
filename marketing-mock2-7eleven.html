    Q1 · Build a SWOT, then choose a marketQ1｜先做 SWOT，再選市場

    Question · 8 points
    CP All is debating whether to keep focusing on 7-Eleven in Thailand or expand faster in Cambodia. Prepare a SWOT analysis for 7-Eleven and use it to recommend which market CP All should prioritize. Contrast the two markets and give at least three reasons.題目｜8 分
    CP All 在「繼續聚焦泰國」與「加速擴張柬埔寨」之間討論。請為 7-Eleven 做 SWOT，並據此建議優先哪個市場。對比兩個市場，至少三個理由。

    • A SWOT table with internal and external items in the right boxes一張內外部分類正確的 SWOT
    • A decision based on the SWOT用 SWOT 做出決定

    ② Which framework (from the lecture slides)② 用哪個框架（老師講義）

    SWOT analysisS4a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： Strengths and weaknesses are internal to the company; opportunities and threats are external, including competitors.優勢與劣勢在公司內部；機會與威脅在外部，包含競爭者。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： The question asks for one, but a SWOT only scores when you use it to decide.題目指定要做，但 SWOT 要用來下決定才會得分。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • S – Strengths (internal, helpful): what we do well, our strongest assets (strong brand, loyal customers, unique tech).S 優勢（內部、有利）：我們做得好的、最強的資產（品牌、忠誠顧客、獨特技術）。
    • W – Weaknesses (internal, harmful): what holds us back (high turnover, debt, weak supply chain, low-performing lines).W 劣勢（內部、不利）：拖累我們的（流動率高、負債、供應鏈弱、表現差的產品線）。
    • O – Opportunities (external, helpful): trends in the market, segments we are not targeting, tariff cuts.O 機會（外部、有利）：市場趨勢、還沒鎖定的客群、關稅下降。
    • T – Threats (external, harmful): competitors and their share, new regulations, rising costs.T 威脅（外部、不利）：競爭者與其市占、新法規、成本上升。
    • The slide says SWOT covers “our own + competitors”. Test for placement: can the company control it? Yes → S/W. No → O/T.講義寫 SWOT 要看「自己＋競爭者」。分類測試：公司能控制嗎？能 → S/W；不能 → O/T。
    • A SWOT is evidence, not an answer: finish by matching strengths to opportunities and saying what you decide.SWOT 是證據不是答案：最後要把優勢對上機會，說出你的決定。

    Product–market growth grid (often called Ansoff)S4aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Four growth routes from existing or new products in existing or new markets; risk rises as you move away from what you already know.用「現有／新產品 × 現有／新市場」分出四種成長路線；離熟悉的越遠，風險越高。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： Market penetration includes “increase usage”, which shows a dense market can still grow.市場滲透包含「增加使用」，說明密集市場仍可成長。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Market penetration (existing product, existing market): increase market share; increase product usage – frequency of use, quantity used, new applications.市場滲透（現有產品、現有市場）：提高市占；增加使用——使用頻率、使用量、新用途。
    • Product development (new product, existing market): product improvement, product-line extensions, new products for the existing market.產品開發（新產品、現有市場）：產品改良、產品線延伸、為現有市場推新產品。
    • Market development (existing product, new market): geographic expansion, targeting a new segment.市場開發（現有產品、新市場）：地理擴張、鎖定新客群。
    • Diversification (new product, new market): forward/backward/horizontal integration; related (concentric) or unrelated (conglomerate).多角化（新產品、新市場）：前向／後向／水平整合；相關（同心）或不相關（集團式）。

    How to use it: name each option with a cell first (“opening more Thai stores is market penetration; Vietnam is market development”), then argue why the extra risk is or is not worth it.答題用法：先用格子替選項命名（「泰國再開店是市場滲透；越南是市場開發」），再論證多出來的風險值不值得。

    Note: the S4a slide shows this grid but does not name it; “Ansoff” is the common textbook name.注意：S4a 投影片有這張表但沒有命名；「Ansoff」是教科書常用名稱。

    ② Which framework (from the lecture slides)② 用哪個框架（老師講義）

    E-commerce vs bricks & mortar · OmnichannelS2★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： E-commerce has clear advantages and disadvantages compared with physical stores; omnichannel joins the channels into one seamless experience.電商相對實體店有明確的好處與壞處；全通路把各通路串成無縫體驗。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： 7-Eleven’s stores can act as delivery points, which fixes e-commerce’s logistics weakness.7-Eleven 門市可當配送點，正好補上電商的物流弱點。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    Good好處

    • Market coverage市場覆蓋廣
    • Higher margins, low investment毛利較高、投資低
    • Learn about customers (clickstream, cookies)了解顧客（點擊紀錄）
    • 24/7; customers interact as much or as little as they want全天候；顧客想互動多少都可以

    Bad壞處

    • Can’t see or touch the physical product看不到、摸不到實物
    • High shipping costs vs cash-and-carry運費高 vs 現場買了帶走
    • Lack of trust: no face, no voice, no store缺乏信任：沒有人、沒有店
    • May conflict with the distribution chain可能和既有通路衝突
    • Merchandising limitations; logistics, including returns (reverse logistics)陳列限制；物流與退貨（逆物流）

    Three models三種模式

    • Bricks & mortar (stores only) · Clicks & mortar (stores + online) · Pure play (100% online)純實體・實體＋線上・純線上

    Omnichannel: using various technologies and channels to give customers a seamless experience (read reviews, check prices, buy, collect).     Six marketing management philosophiesS1Support輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Six ways a company can think about marketing, from production-focused to societal and strategic marketing.公司看待行銷的六種思維，從重生產到社會行銷與策略行銷。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： The societal marketing concept gives the ethical test: short-term gain vs long-term harm.社會行銷觀念提供倫理判準：短期利益 vs 長期傷害。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Production concept: customers want products that are available and affordable → focus on production and distribution efficiency (Ford Model T, “any color as long as it’s black”).生產觀念：顧客要買得到、買得起 → 專注生產與配銷效率（福特 T 型車）。
    • Product concept: customers want the most quality and features → keep improving the product. Risk: marketing myopia and constant me-too copying.產品觀念：顧客要最好品質與功能 → 不斷改良產品。風險：行銷近視、不斷被模仿。
    • Selling concept: people won’t buy enough unless pushed by selling and promotion; common for unsought goods.銷售觀念：不強力推銷顧客就不會買夠；常見於非尋求品。
    • Marketing concept: understand the needs and wants of target markets and satisfy them better than competitors. Many firms claim it but don’t commit.行銷觀念：了解目標市場的需要與想要，做得比對手好。很多公司說有，其實沒做到。
    • Societal marketing concept: questions whether the marketing concept is enough, because it may focus on short-term gains despite long-term effects on people and society.社會行銷觀念：質疑只滿足需要是否足夠，因為可能只顧短期利益、忽略對人與社會的長期影響。
    • Strategic marketing concept: when offers are similar in quality, price and service, the firm with the better profit margin wins; logistics takes cost out of the system (Coke vs Pepsi).策略行銷觀念：品質價格服務都差不多時，毛利較好的贏；用物流降低成本（可口可樂 vs 百事）。

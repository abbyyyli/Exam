    1. Read the complete case1. 閱讀完整案例

    Northstar is a business software implementation company in Bangkok with 24 employees. Staff organize client data, configure systems and complete system launches.Northstar 是位於曼谷、共有 24 名員工的企業軟體導入公司。員工協助顧客整理資料、設定系統並完成上線。

    Six months ago, Mira became operations manager. Better scheduling and a cleared backlog increased on-time completion from 72% to 94%. The owner praised this result, but other concerns have emerged.六個月前，Mira 接任營運主管。她改善排程並清理積壓案件，使準時完成率從 72% 提高到 94%。老闆肯定成果，但也開始收到其他訊號。

    1Mira’s working styleMira 的工作方式

    Mira enjoys bringing people together to discuss new services. She often develops her ideas while talking. She usually starts with future possibilities and leaves the detailed implementation procedures to her colleagues.Mira 喜歡召集大家討論新服務，常在交談中形成想法。她通常先談未來可能性，把詳細執行流程交給同事處理。

    When choosing between options, she asks for comparisons of costs, profits and test results. Even close to a system launch, she may change direction: “If there is a better option, why should we decide so early?”選擇方案時，她要求比較成本、利潤與測試結果。即使接近上線，她仍可能改變方向：「既然有更好的選項，為什麼要太早定案？」

    She says, “I know that I like thinking aloud and that my ideas change quickly.” When staff explain that frequent changes create rework, she replies, “You should be more flexible.”她說：「我知道自己喜歡邊說邊想，想法也變得快。」但員工反映頻繁更動造成重工時，她回答：「你們應該更靈活。」

    2Company promises and employee experience公司承諾與員工經驗

    The company has always emphasized “autonomy, learning and reliable service.” Ana joined because she strongly values learning new skills and solving problems independently.公司一直強調「自主、學習、可靠服務」。Ana 特別重視學習新技能與獨立解決問題，因此加入公司。

    Mira has suspended training and asked everyone to focus on closing more cases. Even small customer remedies require her approval.Mira 暫停培訓，要求大家先提高結案量。小額顧客補救方案也必須由她批准。

    Ana says, “I still enjoy solving clients’ problems, but the way the company works now no longer reflects the company I identified with.” She still completes assigned work, but no longer volunteers to help new employees.Ana 說：「我還是喜歡幫顧客解決問題，但公司現在的做法已經不像我認同的那家公司。」她仍完成份內工作，卻不再主動協助新人。

    3Job design and rewards工作與獎勵安排

    Previously, one employee managed a client’s entire implementation. The work is now divided into small, repetitive steps, and each employee handles only one part. Staff rarely learn whether the client’s launch succeeds or how their own work affects the result.原本一名員工負責一個顧客的完整導入；現在工作拆成重複的小步驟，每人只處理一部分。員工很少知道顧客最後是否順利上線，也不清楚自己的工作如何影響結果。

    Each employee must close 30 cases per week. Mira set the target without discussing differences in case difficulty. Individual closure rankings are published daily, but there is little discussion of quality or improvement.每人每週必須結案 30 件。Mira 自行設定目標，未討論不同案件的難度。每天公布個人結案量排名，但很少討論品質或改善方法。

    Employees earn THB 500 for every ten completed cases. Bonuses are paid together at month end. Once earned, a bonus is not withdrawn even if a case is later reopened. Helping colleagues does not count toward the reward.每完成十件案件，員工獲得 THB 500，月底一起發放。即使案件後來重開，已取得的獎勵也不撤回。協助同事沒有計入獎勵。

    Reopened cases have increased from four to eleven per hundred cases.重新開啟的案件從每百件 4 件增加到 11 件。

    4Different employee responses員工出現不同反應

    Ana suggests testing a new process that would maintain efficiency and reduce rework. Another employee resigns, saying that there are no opportunities for growth.Ana 提出試行新流程，希望保留效率並減少重工。另一名員工辭職，表示自己看不到成長機會。

    Ben prefers clear, stable procedures and finds the new checklists helpful. He believes management will improve the situation, so he continues to support the company and waits.Ben 喜歡明確、穩定的程序，認為新檢查表很有幫助。他相信管理層會改善問題，因此繼續支持公司並等待。

    Another employee deliberately delays replies to clients and does only the minimum required.另有一名員工開始故意拖延回覆顧客，只完成最低要求。

    5A team meeting一次團隊會議

    After Mira makes a last-minute change in priorities, an employee explains that two deadlines now conflict. She interrupts him in front of the team: “If you cannot even handle this, perhaps you are not right for this role.”Mira 臨時調整優先順序後，一名員工表示兩個截止時間互相衝突。她當眾打斷：「如果連這個都處理不了，也許你不適合這個職位。」

    Ana later suggests that Mira should first listen to the employee’s difficulties. Mira replies, “I am maintaining standards. I do not need you to tell me how to manage.”Ana 事後建議她先聽完員工的困難。Mira 回答：「我是在維持標準，不需要你教我怎麼管理。」

    Afterward, employees raise fewer concerns during meetings. No one volunteers to lead the new improvement project.之後，員工比較少在會議提出問題，也沒有人自願帶領新的改善專案。

    6The company’s direction公司的方向

    Mira is good at presenting new ideas to clients and has improved delivery discipline. However, staff hear a new slogan every week and do not know what takes priority when speed, quality and development conflict.Mira 很擅長向顧客介紹新構想，也確實提高交付紀律。但員工說每週都有新口號，卻不知道速度、品質與發展衝突時，究竟應優先考慮什麼。

    The owner asks Mira for a plan that preserves the gains in on-time delivery while improving quality, cooperation and retention.老闆請 Mira 提出改善方案，保留準時交付的成果，同時改善品質、合作與留任。

    2. Facts first, then a useful lens 選框架2. 先看事實，再選適合視角

    A fact is reported in the case. An inference explains it through a concept. An assumption supplies a needed premise that the case does not state. Keep these separate.事實是文章已記載的內容；推論用概念解釋它；假設則補上文章未說、但分析需要的前提。三者分開寫。

    中文邏輯同一個事實可支持不同框架。重點是說清楚理論如何解釋問題，不是把事實配到唯一標準答案。

    3. S1 · Personality and people management3. S1・人格與人員管理

    Mira’s scheduling has helped delivery. However, job design, rewards, people and culture also affect performance. Personality is one part of the explanation.Mira 的排程有助交付，但工作設計、獎勵、人員及文化也會影響績效。人格只是其中一部分。

    Management element管理面向Case evidence個案對應
    Leader領導者Mira’s preferences, decisions, communication and response to criticism.Mira 的偏好、判斷、溝通及回應批評的方式。
    People人員Ana values learning and autonomy; Ben prefers stable procedures.Ana 重視學習及自主；Ben 偏好穩定程序。
    Process and culture流程與文化Fragmented jobs, daily rankings, weak quality discussion and less employee voice.工作切碎、每日排名、少談品質，員工發言減少。
    Outcomes成果Faster delivery, more reopened cases, less helping and a resignation linked to weak growth.交付更快，重開案增加，互助減少，且有人因成長不足離職。

    A supported interpretation: ENTP 偏好有依據的推論：ENTP

    Assume these repeated work habits reflect her usual preferences. Each letter needs its own evidence.假設這些反覆出現的工作習慣反映她平常的偏好。每個字母都需要自己的證據。

    E
    Extraversion外向

    She develops ideas through group discussion. Discussion is a strength; speaking before fully listening is a possible overuse.她透過團體討論形成想法。討論是優勢；還沒聽完就先回應，則可能是過度使用。

    N
    Intuition直覺

    She starts with future possibilities and leaves detailed procedures to others. New ideas help; implementation details can be missed.她先看未來可能性，把細節流程交給別人。新想法有幫助，但可能漏看執行細節。

    T
    Thinking思考

    She compares costs, profits and test results. Clear criteria help decisions; the effect on people still needs attention.她比較成本、利潤及測試結果。明確判準有助決策，但仍須考慮對人的影響。

    P
    Perceiving知覺

    She keeps options open and changes direction near launch. Flexibility helps, but late changes can create rework.她保留選項，接近上線仍改方向。彈性有幫助，但太晚改變可能造成重工。

    Improved scheduling does not automatically make her J. Planning is a skill; keeping options open can remain a preference. T does not explain public threats: examine that conduct through EQ.改善排程不代表她一定偏 J。規劃是能力，保留選項仍可能是偏好。T 也不能解釋公開威脅；這類行為應用 EQ 分析。

    Awareness of preferences對偏好的覺察

    She knows she thinks aloud and changes ideas quickly. The case does not say that she knows her full type.她知道自己邊說邊想、想法變得快，但文章沒有說她知道完整類型。

    Awareness of impact對影響的覺察

    She dismisses the rework caused by her changes. Understanding her impact on others is weaker.她忽視自己改變方向造成的重工，對他人所受影響的理解較弱。

    Ben’s stable-procedure preference is a J-like clue, not a full type. Ana’s wish for autonomy is clearer evidence of a value than an MBTI type.Ben 喜歡穩定程序，是偏 J 的線索，不是完整類型。Ana 希望自主，更清楚反映價值觀，而非 MBTI 類型。

    Mira improves schedules but keeps options open near launch. Why can both facts fit a P-like preference?Mira 改善排程，卻在接近上線時仍保留選項。為何兩項事實仍可與偏 P 的推論相容？

    4. S2 · Values, attitudes and behavior4. S2・價值觀、態度與行為

    Stated values versus daily practice 說做不一宣稱價值與日常做法

    Autonomy自主

    Small customer remedies need Mira’s approval. Daily practice leaves little room for independent decisions.小型顧客補救也要 Mira 核准，日常做法留下的自主決策空間很小。

    Learning學習

    Training stops while staff are told to close more cases.培訓停止，員工被要求先提高結案量。

    Reliable service可靠服務

    The company promises reliable service, but its policy pays for closure counts even when cases later reopen. The reward policy does not fully support the stated value.公司承諾可靠服務，但政策依結案數付獎金，即使案件後來重開也一樣。獎勵政策沒有充分支持宣稱的價值。

    The same fact can support three different arguments. The framework determines the question being asked.同一事實可以支持三種不同論證。採用的框架決定分析時問什麼。

    Lens分析視角What this fact means這項事實代表什麼
    Values價值觀Reliable service is the stated value, but the reward policy gives little weight to lasting quality: a stated-practice discrepancy.宣稱重視可靠服務，獎勵政策卻較少考慮持續品質：這是說做不一。
    Goal setting / MBO目標設定／MBOThirty closures per week measures quantity, not whether clients’ problems stay solved: weak alignment between the target and the intended result.每週結案 30 件衡量數量，未衡量顧客問題是否持續解決：目標與真正成果對齊不足。
    Reinforcement增強理論Ten closures earn THB 500 even if cases reopen: the consequence rewards closure volume more directly than lasting quality.十件結案就獲 THB 500，即使重開也不撤回：後果更直接獎勵結案量，而非持續品質。

    Ana agrees with the stated values. The clearer problem is a stated-practice discrepancy: current practices do not support autonomy and learning. Mira’s actions give greater weight to speed, output and control.Ana 認同宣稱的價值。更明顯的問題是說做不一：目前做法未支持自主及學習。Mira 的行動更重視速度、產出及控制。

    Ana values learning and independence. Ben prefers stable procedures. The same arrangement can help one employee and frustrate another.Ana 重視學習與獨立；Ben 偏好穩定程序。同一安排可能幫助一人，卻讓另一人受挫。

    Separate Ana’s attitude from her behavior 態度與行為分開 Ana 的態度與行為

    Component成分What the case supports個案支持的判斷

    Affective情感She still enjoys client problem-solving; her statement suggests disappointment with the organization.她仍喜歡解決顧客問題；她的話也顯示對組織失望。

    Daily rankings communicate “close more cases.” Public criticism suggests that raising concerns may be risky. These daily signals compete with the stated values. Mira shows some A-like impatience and performance pressure, but the case is not enough for a full A/B classification.每日排名傳達「多結案」；公開批評暗示提出問題可能有風險。這些日常訊號與宣稱價值衝突。Mira 有部分偏 A 的急躁及績效壓力，但資料不足以做完整 A／B 分類。

    5. S3 · Needs, management style and goals 動機5. S3・需求、管理方式與目標

    The bonus is extrinsic. Ana’s enjoyment of solving client problems is intrinsic. External rewards do not directly replace learning, independence or meaningful work.獎金是外在獎勵；Ana 喜歡解決顧客問題，屬於內在動機。外在獎勵不能直接取代學習、自主或有意義的工作。

    Maslow: assess all five needsMaslow：逐項判斷五種需求

    Need需求Evidence → assessment證據 → 判斷
    Physiological生理Bonuses provide material rewards. Basic pay and living needs are not described enough to judge full support.獎金提供物質報酬；基本薪資及生活需求資料不足，無法判斷是否充分支持。
    Safety安全Mira publicly questions whether an employee fits the role. If staff interpret this as a job threat, insecurity is a possible gap.Mira 公開質疑員工是否適任。若員工視為工作威脅，不安全感可能構成缺口。
    Social / belonging社交／歸屬Fragmented work, less helping and poor responses to concerns weaken cooperation and support.片段工作、互助減少及不當回應疑慮，削弱合作與支持。
    Esteem尊重Rankings recognize output, but public criticism harms respect. Quality and helping receive little recognition.排名肯定產出，但公開批評傷害尊重；品質與助人貢獻較少受到肯定。
    Self-actualization自我實現Training stops and independent problem-solving is restricted. One employee leaves because growth is weak.培訓停止，獨立解題受限；一位員工因成長不足離職。

    ERG: several needs can matter at the same timeERG：多種需求可同時重要

    ERG groups needs into Existence, Relatedness and Growth. A financial reward can support one group while the other groups remain weak.ERG 將需求分為生存、關係及成長。金錢獎勵可以支持其中一類，其他需求仍可能不足。

    Need and meaning需求及意義Case fact → assessment個案事實 → 判斷
    Existence: material basics生存：基本物質需求THB 500 per ten cases provides a financial reward. This supports material needs, but base pay is not given, so full adequacy cannot be judged.每十件有 THB 500，提供金錢報酬，支持物質需求。但基本薪資未交代，不能判斷是否充分。
    Relatedness: supportive relationships關係：相互支持的人際關係Mira rejects concerns, staff speak less and Ana stops helping newcomers. These facts show weaker support and cooperation, which are relatedness gaps.Mira 拒絕疑慮，員工發言減少，Ana 停止協助新人。支持及合作變弱，對應關係需求缺口。
    Growth: developing and using abilities成長：發展並運用能力Training stops, jobs become small repeated steps and independent problem-solving is restricted. Staff have fewer chances to learn and use their abilities: a growth gap.培訓停止，工作變成重複小步驟，獨立解題受限。員工學習及運用能力的機會減少，對應成長缺口。

    ERG diagnosis: financial rewards coexist with relationship and growth gaps. These needs can matter together; receiving money does not remove the other needs.ERG 診斷：金錢獎勵與關係、成長缺口並存。這些需求可以同時重要，拿到金錢不會使其他需求消失。

    Frustration-regression requires a shift toward lower needs after growth is blocked. The case shows blocked growth, but not that shift.挫折退化需要顯示：成長受阻後，員工轉向較低層需求。案例有成長受阻，但未顯示這個轉向。

    Herzberg: dissatisfaction and motivation are differentHerzberg：不滿與動機是兩回事

    Hygiene factors are conditions around the job; poor supervision or policy can create dissatisfaction. Motivators are growth, responsibility, achievement and recognition in the work itself.保健因素是工作周圍的條件；督導或政策不佳可能造成不滿。激勵因素則是工作本身的成長、責任、成就及肯定。

    Factor因素Case fact → assessment個案事實 → 判斷
    Hygiene: supervision and policy保健：督導與政策Mira publicly questions an employee’s suitability and dismisses Ana’s feedback. Even small remedies need approval. Poor treatment and restrictive policy can make the job frustrating.Mira 公開質疑員工是否適任，並拒絕 Ana 的回饋。小型補救也須核准。不佳對待及限制性政策可能使員工不滿。
    Hygiene: pay保健：薪酬A bonus is provided. This does not fix poor supervision, and the case gives no basis for judging base-pay adequacy.有提供獎金，但獎金不會修好不佳督導；文章也不足以判斷基本薪資是否足夠。
    Motivators: growth and responsibility激勵：成長與責任Suspended training reduces learning. Fragmented jobs and approval rules reduce ownership and independent decisions. These weaken growth and responsibility.培訓停止減少學習；片段工作及核准規則減少完整責任與獨立決策，削弱成長及責任。
    Motivators: achievement and recognition激勵：成就與肯定Rankings recognize case numbers, but staff rarely see successful client launches. Recognition is narrow and staff have less information about the value of their completed work.排名肯定結案數，但員工很少看到顧客成功上線。肯定偏窄，員工也較難知道完成的工作產生什麼價值。

    Herzberg diagnosis: poor treatment can create dissatisfaction, while restricted growth and ownership weaken motivation. A bonus alone does not solve either problem.Herzberg 診斷：不佳對待可能造成不滿；成長及完整責任受限，則削弱動機。單靠獎金不能解決這兩種問題。

    ERG identifies which needs are weak. Herzberg explains why reducing dissatisfaction and building motivation require different conditions.ERG 指出哪些需求不足；Herzberg 說明減少不滿與建立動機，需要不同條件。

    Theory X/Y: belief → style → fitX／Y 理論：假設 → 做法 → 配適

    Theory X assumptionX 理論假設

    Staff need external control and pressure because they avoid work or responsibility.員工會逃避工作或責任，因此需要外在控制及壓力。

    Theory Y assumptionY 理論假設

    Staff can accept responsibility and direct their own work when committed to clear goals.員工認同清楚目標時，可以承擔責任並自我引導工作。

    Use the same regular team review where possible. Five factors do not require five separate meeting programs. The owner should review behavior, staff feedback and delivery results together.可以盡量運用同一個固定團隊檢討。五個因素不代表需要五套會議。老闆應一起檢視行為、員工回饋及交付成果。

    Q6 · You can recommend only three priority actions to the owner. What would you choose?Q6・只能向老闆提出三項優先行動，你會選什麼？

    Combine the analysis into one plan. Explain the problem each action addresses, how the actions work together and which of Mira’s strengths should be retained.把分析整合成計畫，說明每項行動處理哪個問題、彼此如何配合，以及應保留 Mira 哪些優點。

    Show model answer展開示範答案

    1 · Align goals and rewards1・讓目標與獎勵對齊

    Define success as timely and reliable completion. Consider case difficulty, and include quality and team results in rewards. This closes the gap between case numbers and reliable service.把成功定義為準時且可靠地完成，考慮案件難度，將品質及團隊成果納入獎勵，處理結案數與可靠服務的落差。

    2 · Restore ownership and learning2・恢復完整責任與學習

    Give staff ownership of complete cases, clear limits for routine decisions and learning opportunities. Return final client results to the people doing the work. This supports autonomy, responsibility, growth and problem-solving.讓員工負責完整案件、在明確界線內做例行決策，並有學習機會。最終顧客成果回到執行者手中，支持自主、責任、成長及解題。

    3 · Change the response to concerns3・改變回應疑慮的方式

    Make respectful correction, listening and follow-through part of Mira’s responsibilities. The owner and coach review real incidents and staff feedback. This helps employees raise problems early and join improvements.將尊重的修正、傾聽及後續處理列入 Mira 的責任。老闆與教練檢視真實事件及員工回饋，讓員工能及早提出問題並參與改善。

    The first action defines the right result. The second gives staff the ability and responsibility to achieve it. The third allows people to speak and contribute. Keep Mira’s scheduling discipline, useful checklists and ability to present ideas. Protect on-time delivery while reducing reopened cases and improving cooperation and retention.第一項定義正確成果；第二項提供達成成果的能力及責任；第三項讓人能發言及貢獻。保留 Mira 的排程紀律、有用的檢查表及介紹構想的能力，保護準時交付，同時減少重開案、改善合作及留任。

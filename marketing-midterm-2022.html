    Evidence rule used in these answers (v7)本版答案的證據原則（v7）

    In the exam you only have the short case. So every answer here is built from two things: facts in the case or the question, and frameworks from the lectures. When a question cannot be answered without something outside the case (for example, it asks you to name a competitor or another country), the outside idea is kept broad, written as an assumption, and turned into something you would check.考試時你手上只有短短的題幹。所以這裡每個答案只用兩種東西：題幹或題目寫的事實，和課堂的框架。如果不用外部資訊就答不了（例如題目要你說出競爭者或另一個國家），外部資訊只寫概略的說法、標成假設，並轉成「要去查證的事」。

    Tags in the answers:答案裡的標籤： Case Course Assumption

    • Case: stated in the case or the question. Use it freely.Case：題幹或題目有寫，可以放心用。
    • Course: a framework or idea from the slides. It is your reasoning tool, not outside data.Course：講義上的框架或觀念，是推理工具，不是外部資料。

    Robinson Department Store Plc, a Central Group company, will spend 4.5 billion baht to expand business next year in hopes to see a rebound in consumer spending.

    The SET-listed retailer plans to open more several stores in Thailand and Vietnam within the next five years. A Robinson executive said three of the Thai stores would be in suburban Bangkok and one each in Rayong and Buriram provinces.

    “We will expand our department stores in secondary provinces, which have huge opportunities because there are no direct competitors to ROBINSON,” he said.

    ROBINSON will open more lifestyle complexes upcountry after good customer response at its first one in Trang province. The company has 52 ROBINSON Department Stores in Thailand, two in Vietnam and three more opening in Thailand by 2023.

    A ROBINSON lifestyle center was opened in Prachin Buri province. Spending 980 million baht on the project with 20,000 square meters of space, it aims to attract young people. The province is an industrial, commercial and transport hub in eastern Thailand and positioned to respond to increased purchasing power with the launch of the ASEAN Economic Community.

    The ROBINSON lifestyle center concept is anchored by Power Buy, Super Sport and Tops Supermarket, plus 30 restaurants and the SF Cinema. The company has also begun expanding their presence online, and via catalogs.

    Pitsinee Jitpleecheep, Bangkok Post. MGMG 508 Strategic Marketing Management, College of Management, Mahidol University. 7 points each, 35 points total.

    ② Which framework (from the lecture slides)② 用哪個框架（老師講義）

    5Cs: Company, Customers, Competitors, Collaborators, ContextS1 · S5a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： The professor’s way to understand a situation before choosing: what the company has, who the customers are and what they want, who else competes, who helps deliver, and what is happening around them.老師分析現況的方法，在做選擇之前先看：公司有什麼、顧客是誰要什麼、還有誰在競爭、誰幫忙交付、周邊環境發生什麼。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： Q1 is a market comparison. The case describes Thailand in some detail but says only one thing about Vietnam: Robinson has two stores there and plans more. So the Thailand column carries most of the evidence, and the Vietnam column shows the foothold plus what must be checked.Q1 是市場比較。題幹寫了不少泰國的事，越南只寫了一件：Robinson 在那裡有兩家店，也計畫再開。所以證據大多在泰國那欄，越南那欄寫既有據點和要查證的事。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Company: strengths, resources, existing stores or products, capabilities.公司：優勢、資源、現有據點或產品、能力。
    • Customers: who they are and what they want – S1’s market size, age and income sit here; needs, wants and demand (wants backed by buying power).顧客：是誰、想要什麼——S1 的市場規模、年齡、所得放這裡；需要、想要、需求（有購買力的想要）。
    • Competitors: direct (same category) and indirect (other ways to meet the same need).競爭者：直接（同品類）與間接（滿足同一需要的其他方式）。
    • Collaborators: suppliers, distributors, retailers, partners, parent group businesses.合作者：供應商、通路、零售商、夥伴、母集團事業。
    • Context: economy and growth, regulation and politics, culture and religion, technology (the macro-environment of S4a).情境：經濟與成長、法規與政治、文化與宗教、科技（即 S4a 的總體環境）。

    5Cs vs SWOT: the same facts in two layouts. Company → Strengths and Weaknesses; Customers, Competitors, Collaborators and Context → Opportunities and Threats. Use SWOT when the question asks for it; otherwise a 5C contrast table is clearer for comparing two markets.5C 與 SWOT：同一批事實的兩種排法。公司 → 優勢與劣勢；顧客、競爭者、合作者、情境 → 機會與威脅。題目要 SWOT 就用 SWOT；比較兩個市場時，5C 對比表比較清楚。

    Where it leads: 5Cs (understand) → STP (choose whom to serve and why they choose us) → 4Ps (deliver). Slides: S1 “The 5Cs, STP and the 4Ps constitute the marketing management framework”; S5a “Marketing Overview”.接下來：5C（了解）→ STP（選擇服務誰、為何選我們）→ 4P（執行）。講義：S1「5C、STP、4P 構成行銷管理架構」；S5a「Marketing Overview」。

    How to use it in an answer: make one row per C with both markets side by side, mark which market wins each row, then turn the rows that matter into your reasons.答題用法：每個 C 一列、兩個市場並排，標出每列誰佔優，再把關鍵的列變成理由。

    Product–market growth grid (often called Ansoff)S4aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Four growth routes from existing or new products in existing or new markets; risk rises as you move away from what you already know.用「現有／新產品 × 現有／新市場」分出四種成長路線；離熟悉的越遠，風險越高。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： More Thai stores = market penetration; Vietnam = market development, which is riskier. Robinson already has stores there, which lowers that risk.泰國再開店＝市場滲透；越南＝市場開發，風險較高。Robinson 在越南已有店，風險因此降低。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Market penetration (existing product, existing market): increase market share; increase product usage – frequency of use, quantity used, new applications.市場滲透（現有產品、現有市場）：提高市占；增加使用——使用頻率、使用量、新用途。
    • Product development (new product, existing market): product improvement, product-line extensions, new products for the existing market.產品開發（新產品、現有市場）：產品改良、產品線延伸、為現有市場推新產品。
    • Market development (existing product, new market): geographic expansion, targeting a new segment.市場開發（現有產品、新市場）：地理擴張、鎖定新客群。
    • Diversification (new product, new market): forward/backward/horizontal integration; related (concentric) or unrelated (conglomerate).多角化（新產品、新市場）：前向／後向／水平整合；相關（同心）或不相關（集團式）。

    How to use it: name each option with a cell first (“opening more Thai stores is market penetration; Vietnam is market development”), then argue why the extra risk is or is not worth it.答題用法：先用格子替選項命名（「泰國再開店是市場滲透；越南是市場開發」），再論證多出來的風險值不值得。

    Note: the S4a slide shows this grid but does not name it; “Ansoff” is the common textbook name.注意：S4a 投影片有這張表但沒有命名；「Ansoff」是教科書常用名稱。

    ⑤ Exam-ready paragraph⑤ 考場段落範例

    Use your own words and cite only the case facts you need as evidence (S5b: “do not repeat parts of the case back to me”).用自己的話，只引用必要的題幹事實當證據（S5b：不要把案例抄回來）。

    I recommend Robinson prioritize Vietnam for its next complexes, but step by step. The case gives Thailand real strengths: 52 stores, a lifestyle format that worked in Trang, and secondary provinces with no direct competitors. Those strengths are already being used through the Thai openings in the plan. For the next priority, Vietnam is the better choice for three reasons. First, Robinson already has two stores there, so it is not starting from zero; in growth-grid terms this is market development, which is riskier than adding Thai stores but less risky than entering a country with no presence. Second, Robinson’s new format is designed to attract young people and it targets lower-income shoppers; assuming Vietnam has a large young mass market, the same format can be used there without changing Robinson’s position. Third, the case links Robinson’s growth to rising purchasing power across ASEAN, and Vietnam is part of that same region. Because the case says little about Vietnamese customers or competitors, I would first study the sales of the two existing stores and check whether the same anchor tenants can open there, and then compare sales per square meter and payback with Thai stores.

    STP · Targeting strategies · PositioningS5aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Segment the market, choose the segments to target, then plan the position the product should hold in customers’ minds compared with competitors.區隔市場、選擇目標客群，再規劃產品相對競爭者在顧客心中的位置。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    Segmentation區隔

    • Bases: geographic, demographic, psychographic, family life cycle.依據：地理、人口統計、心理統計、家庭生命週期。
    • Effective segments are measurable (size, purchasing power), accessible (can be reached), substantial (large and profitable enough), actionable (can be served).有效區隔要可衡量、可接觸、夠大、可執行。

    Targeting選目標

    • Consider current and potential competitors, threat of substitutes, buyer and supplier power, and the company’s objectives and resources.考慮現有與潛在競爭者、替代品威脅、買方與供應商議價力、公司目標與資源。
    • Undifferentiated: one product for everyone (gum). Differentiated: separate offers for different segments – more sales but higher costs (Breeze, Omo, Sunsilk, Dove).     • A product’s position is where it sits in the consumer’s mind compared with competing products. Don’t leave it to chance.定位是產品相對對手在顧客心中的位置，不要放任自然形成。
    • Three steps: (1) identify possible competitive advantages; (2) select the right one(s) – one difference = USP; (3) communicate and deliver it – the marketing mix must support it.三步：(1) 找出可能的競爭優勢；(2) 選對的（一個差異＝USP）；(3) 傳達並做到——行銷組合要支持。

    • Risk types: product performance risk, social risk, financial risk. How big? It depends on category and situation.風險類型：產品表現風險、社會風險、財務風險；大小取決於品類與情境。
    • Evoked set = all brands you know; consideration set = brands you would consider buying.喚起集合＝你知道的所有品牌；考慮名單＝你會考慮買的品牌。
    • Cognitive dissonance: anxiety or regret after a difficult, expensive or long-term decision; advertising can reassure buyers.認知失調：困難、昂貴或長期承諾的決定後的焦慮或後悔；廣告可以安撫。

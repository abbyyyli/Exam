    Evidence rule used in these answers (v7)本版答案的證據原則（v7）

    In the exam you only have the short case. So every answer here is built from two things: facts in the case or the question, and frameworks from the lectures. When a question cannot be answered without something outside the case (for example, it asks you to name a competitor or another country), the outside idea is kept broad, written as an assumption, and turned into something you would check.考試時你手上只有短短的題幹。所以這裡每個答案只用兩種東西：題幹或題目寫的事實，和課堂的框架。如果不用外部資訊就答不了（例如題目要你說出競爭者或另一個國家），外部資訊只寫概略的說法、標成假設，並轉成「要去查證的事」。

    The case題幹

    Case (news-style short case, as in the exam)Case（新聞短案例，和考試一樣）

    Sugar tax and a fresher rival put pressure on M-150

    Osotspa’s M-150 is Thailand’s leading energy drink, sold at about 10 baht a bottle, mainly through small grocery shops. Osotspa also owns Shark, a smaller energy drink in Thailand, and C-vitt, which leads Thailand’s fast-growing vitamin-C drink category. Its core customers are manual workers, drivers and farmers. Rival Carabao Dang uses football sponsorship to build a modern image, and ready-to-drink coffee such as Birdy also competes for workers’ energy needs.

    Thailand’s sugar tax and growing health concerns are pressuring sweet drinks. Osotspa has a factory in Myanmar and sells in Cambodia and Laos. Management is considering overseas growth, more online sales, lower-sugar products and whether M-150 should appeal to urban office workers.

    Practice case written from public information; figures are approximate and treated as case facts.練習用個案，依公開資訊改寫；數字為約略值，練習時視為題幹事實。

    Key facts to use as evidence可當證據的題幹重點

    • M-150 leads Thai energy drinks, about 10 baht, sold mainly in grocery shops; Osotspa also owns Shark (a small energy drink in Thailand) and C-vitt (leader in fast-growing vitamin-C drinks).M-150 是泰國能量飲料龍頭，約 10 銖，主要在雜貨店賣；Osotspa 另有 Shark（在泰國是小能量飲料）與 C-vitt（快速成長的維他命 C 飲料龍頭）。
    • Core customers: manual workers, drivers, farmers.核心客群：勞工、司機、農民。
    • Rivals: Carabao (football, modern image); Birdy coffee competes for energy needs.對手：Carabao（足球、現代形象）；Birdy 咖啡搶提神需求。
    • Sugar tax and health concerns pressure sweet drinks.糖稅與健康意識壓迫含糖飲料。
    • Factory in Myanmar; sells in Cambodia and Laos.緬甸有工廠；賣柬埔寨與寮國。

    ② Which framework (from the lecture slides)② 用哪個框架（老師講義）

    5Cs: Company, Customers, Competitors, Collaborators, ContextS1 · S5a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： The professor’s way to understand a situation before choosing: what the company has, who the customers are and what they want, who else competes, who helps deliver, and what is happening around them.老師分析現況的方法，在做選擇之前先看：公司有什麼、顧客是誰要什麼、還有誰在競爭、誰幫忙交付、周邊環境發生什麼。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： Compare Thailand and the chosen country across the 5Cs. The case describes Thailand (market leader, sugar tax, health concerns) and only says where Osotspa already operates abroad, so build the contrast on that and turn the rest into checks.用 5C 比較泰國與選定國家。題幹詳細寫了泰國（龍頭、糖稅、健康意識），國外只寫了 Osotspa 在哪裡有據點，所以對比就建立在這些上面，其餘轉成要查證的事。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Company: strengths, resources, existing stores or products, capabilities.公司：優勢、資源、現有據點或產品、能力。
    • Customers: who they are and what they want – S1’s market size, age and income sit here; needs, wants and demand (wants backed by buying power).顧客：是誰、想要什麼——S1 的市場規模、年齡、所得放這裡；需要、想要、需求（有購買力的想要）。
    • Competitors: direct (same category) and indirect (other ways to meet the same need).競爭者：直接（同品類）與間接（滿足同一需要的其他方式）。
    • Collaborators: suppliers, distributors, retailers, partners, parent group businesses.合作者：供應商、通路、零售商、夥伴、母集團事業。
    • Context: economy and growth, regulation and politics, culture and religion, technology (the macro-environment of S4a).情境：經濟與成長、法規與政治、文化與宗教、科技（即 S4a 的總體環境）。

    5Cs vs SWOT: the same facts in two layouts. Company → Strengths and Weaknesses; Customers, Competitors, Collaborators and Context → Opportunities and Threats. Use SWOT when the question asks for it; otherwise a 5C contrast table is clearer for comparing two markets.5C 與 SWOT：同一批事實的兩種排法。公司 → 優勢與劣勢；顧客、競爭者、合作者、情境 → 機會與威脅。題目要 SWOT 就用 SWOT；比較兩個市場時，5C 對比表比較清楚。

    Where it leads: 5Cs (understand) → STP (choose whom to serve and why they choose us) → 4Ps (deliver). Slides: S1 “The 5Cs, STP and the 4Ps constitute the marketing management framework”; S5a “Marketing Overview”.接下來：5C（了解）→ STP（選擇服務誰、為何選我們）→ 4P（執行）。講義：S1「5C、STP、4P 構成行銷管理架構」；S5a「Marketing Overview」。

    How to use it in an answer: make one row per C with both markets side by side, mark which market wins each row, then turn the rows that matter into your reasons.答題用法：每個 C 一列、兩個市場並排，標出每列誰佔優，再把關鍵的列變成理由。

    SWOT analysisS4aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： Strengths and weaknesses are internal to the company; opportunities and threats are external, including competitors.優勢與劣勢在公司內部；機會與威脅在外部，包含競爭者。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： S6: the professor may ask for “a country comparison or a SWOT”. Here you see the same facts rearranged as a SWOT, so you can answer either way.S6：老師可能考「國家比較或 SWOT」。這裡把同一批事實重排成 SWOT，兩種問法都能答。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • S – Strengths (internal, helpful): what we do well, our strongest assets (strong brand, loyal customers, unique tech).S 優勢（內部、有利）：我們做得好的、最強的資產（品牌、忠誠顧客、獨特技術）。
    • W – Weaknesses (internal, harmful): what holds us back (high turnover, debt, weak supply chain, low-performing lines).W 劣勢（內部、不利）：拖累我們的（流動率高、負債、供應鏈弱、表現差的產品線）。
    • O – Opportunities (external, helpful): trends in the market, segments we are not targeting, tariff cuts.O 機會（外部、有利）：市場趨勢、還沒鎖定的客群、關稅下降。
    • T – Threats (external, harmful): competitors and their share, new regulations, rising costs.T 威脅（外部、不利）：競爭者與其市占、新法規、成本上升。
    • The slide says SWOT covers “our own + competitors”. Test for placement: can the company control it? Yes → S/W. No → O/T.講義寫 SWOT 要看「自己＋競爭者」。分類測試：公司能控制嗎？能 → S/W；不能 → O/T。
    • A SWOT is evidence, not an answer: finish by matching strengths to opportunities and saying what you decide.SWOT 是證據不是答案：最後要把優勢對上機會，說出你的決定。

    ⑤ Exam-ready paragraph⑤ 考場段落範例

    Use your own words and cite only the case facts you need as evidence (S5b: “do not repeat parts of the case back to me”).用自己的話，只引用必要的題幹事實當證據（S5b：不要把案例抄回來）。

    I recommend Osotspa prioritize Cambodia and grow there step by step. The case shows that Thailand is becoming difficult: M-150 already leads the energy-drink market, Carabao and Birdy compete for the same workers, and the sugar tax and health concerns are squeezing sweet drinks. First, because M-150 already leads at home, there is little share left to win, so Osotspa needs another source of growth. Second, M-150 is already sold in Cambodia, so this is market development with an existing foothold, which carries less risk than entering a country where Osotspa has no presence. Third, the case mentions the sugar tax only in Thailand, so growing in Cambodia spreads that risk, provided research confirms that Cambodian rules are different. The Myanmar factory could help supply, but the case gives no information on sales there. Because the case says little about Cambodian customers and rivals, I would first check sales trends and the size of the manual-worker market, compare Cambodia with Laos on the same measures, and then track outlet numbers and market share.

    ② Which framework (from the lecture slides)② 用哪個框架（老師講義）

    BCG growth–share matrix · Product life cycleS4a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： Classifies business units or products by market growth rate and relative market share into Stars, Question Marks, Cash Cows and Dogs.用市場成長率與相對市占，把事業單位或產品分成明星、問號、金牛、狗。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： The question names the tool. Place each brand by market growth and relative market share, then use the four strategies (build, hold, harvest, divest).題目指定工具。依市場成長率與相對市占放每個品牌，再用四種策略（建立、維持、收割、撤資）。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    Direct vs Indirect competitionS5b★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： Direct competitors offer the same category to the same target; indirect competitors offer a different product that can satisfy the same need.直接競爭者在同品類、同客群；間接競爭者提供不同產品，但能滿足同一個需要。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： Carabao (energy drink) is direct; Birdy canned coffee is indirect (it also gives energy).Carabao（能量飲料）是直接；Birdy 罐裝咖啡是間接（也能提神）。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Direct: same category and market, usually the same target (Coca-Cola vs Pepsi).直接：同品類、同市場，通常同客群（可口可樂 vs 百事）。
    • Indirect: different products that can be substituted to fulfil the same need (Coca-Cola vs Lipton tea). Even time is contested: TV, internet, games compete for entertainment.間接：不同產品但可替代、滿足同一需要（可口可樂 vs 立頓茶）。連時間也在競爭：電視、網路、遊戲都搶娛樂時間。
    • Practical test (S6): if a customer could choose it instead of yours for that occasion, it is a competitor. Name the category and occasion first.實用判準（S6）：顧客在那個情境可以改買它，它就是競爭者。先講清楚品類與情境。
    • Market types: pure monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition, pure competition. Entry barriers (hard to start) and exit barriers (hard to leave).市場類型：獨占、寡占、壟斷性競爭、完全競爭。進入障礙與退出障礙。
    • Customer value analysis: value = benefits − costs; consumers may not be that rational.顧客價值分析：價值＝利益 − 成本；顧客不一定那麼理性。
    • Competitive strategies (S5b): offense vs defense; market leader vs follower (technology); competitive intelligence – analyse competitors and trends, but don’t spend so much that you become myopic.競爭策略（S5b）：攻擊 vs 防守；領導者 vs 跟隨者；競爭情報——分析對手與趨勢，但別花太多變成近視。
    • Brand stretching: when a brand extends into a new category, ask whether the new products are direct or indirect competitors of existing ones.品牌延伸：品牌跨入新品類時，要問新產品和現有產品是直接還是間接競爭。

    Six marketing management philosophiesS1★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： Six ways a company can think about marketing, from production-focused to societal and strategic marketing.公司看待行銷的六種思維，從重生產到社會行銷與策略行銷。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Production concept: customers want products that are available and affordable → focus on production and distribution efficiency (Ford Model T, “any color as long as it’s black”).生產觀念：顧客要買得到、買得起 → 專注生產與配銷效率（福特 T 型車）。
    • Product concept: customers want the most quality and features → keep improving the product. Risk: marketing myopia and constant me-too copying.產品觀念：顧客要最好品質與功能 → 不斷改良產品。風險：行銷近視、不斷被模仿。
    • Selling concept: people won’t buy enough unless pushed by selling and promotion; common for unsought goods.銷售觀念：不強力推銷顧客就不會買夠；常見於非尋求品。
    • Marketing concept: understand the needs and wants of target markets and satisfy them better than competitors. Many firms claim it but don’t commit.行銷觀念：了解目標市場的需要與想要，做得比對手好。很多公司說有，其實沒做到。
    • Societal marketing concept: questions whether the marketing concept is enough, because it may focus on short-term gains despite long-term effects on people and society.社會行銷觀念：質疑只滿足需要是否足夠，因為可能只顧短期利益、忽略對人與社會的長期影響。
    • Strategic marketing concept: when offers are similar in quality, price and service, the firm with the better profit margin wins; logistics takes cost out of the system (Coke vs Pepsi).策略行銷觀念：品質價格服務都差不多時，毛利較好的贏；用物流降低成本（可口可樂 vs 百事）。

    STP · Targeting strategies · PositioningS5a★ Write this in the exam★ 考試寫這個

    What it is:是什麼： Segment the market, choose the segments to target, then plan the position the product should hold in customers’ minds compared with competitors.區隔市場、選擇目標客群，再規劃產品相對競爭者在顧客心中的位置。

    Why this question uses it:這題為什麼用它： Two tools from S5a: differentiated marketing (serve a new segment with a separate offer) and positioning errors (confused positioning, avoid abrupt changes).兩個 S5a 工具：差異化行銷（用另一個產品服務新區隔）與定位錯誤（定位混亂、避免突然改變）。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）

    Segmentation區隔

    • Bases: geographic, demographic, psychographic, family life cycle.依據：地理、人口統計、心理統計、家庭生命週期。
    • Effective segments are measurable (size, purchasing power), accessible (can be reached), substantial (large and profitable enough), actionable (can be served).有效區隔要可衡量、可接觸、夠大、可執行。

    Targeting選目標

    • Consider current and potential competitors, threat of substitutes, buyer and supplier power, and the company’s objectives and resources.考慮現有與潛在競爭者、替代品威脅、買方與供應商議價力、公司目標與資源。
    • Undifferentiated: one product for everyone (gum). Differentiated: separate offers for different segments – more sales but higher costs (Breeze, Omo, Sunsilk, Dove). Concentrated: a large share of one niche – risk of all eggs in one basket.無差異：一個產品給所有人。差異化：不同區隔不同產品，銷售多但成本高（Breeze、Omo）。集中：在一個利基拿高市占，風險集中。

    Positioning定位

    • A product’s position is where it sits in the consumer’s mind compared with competing products. Don’t leave it to chance.定位是產品相對對手在顧客心中的位置，不要放任自然形成。
    • Three steps: (1) identify possible competitive advantages; (2) select the right one(s) – one difference = USP; (3) communicate and deliver it – the marketing mix must support it.三步：(1) 找出可能的競爭優勢；(2) 選對的（一個差異＝USP）；(3) 傳達並做到——行銷組合要支持。
    • Differentiation types: product, services, personnel (better-trained people), image (symbols, colours, celebrities). Most advantages are temporary.差異化類型：產品、服務、人員（訓練更好的員工）、形象（符號、顏色、名人）。多數優勢是暫時的。
    • Errors: underpositioning (no clear idea), overpositioning (too narrow), confused positioning (mixed image).錯誤：定位不足、定位過窄、定位混亂。
    • Avoid abrupt changes: they confuse consumers, who may resist; a position should evolve gradually.避免突然改變：顧客會困惑、抗拒；定位應逐步演進。

    The professor’s caution: don’t believe in STP too much – anyone can buy anything, and most people are not 100% loyal.老師提醒：別太迷信 STP，任何人都可能買任何東西，多數人也不是 100% 忠誠。

    4Ps (marketing mix)S1 · S4a · S5aSupport輔助

    What it is:是什麼： The controllable tools – Product, Price, Place, Promotion – that deliver and communicate the chosen position to the target.可控制的工具——產品、價格、通路、推廣——把選定的定位傳遞並溝通給目標客群。

    Framework explained (from the slides)框架詳解（老師講義）
    • Product: features, functions and benefits; how customers use it; how it differs from competitors; branding and positioning.產品：功能與利益、顧客怎麼用、和對手有何不同、品牌與定位。

    • Promotion: which segments need engagement; where customers look for information; timing; cost-effective channels.推廣：哪些客群要經營、顧客在哪找資訊、時機、有效率的管道。
    • S4a: the 4Ps are “the controllable variables the firm blends to produce the response it wants in the target market” (People? added). S5a: “the marketing mix must support the strategy” – action, not just talk.S4a：4P 是「公司組合起來、在目標市場產生想要反應的可控變數」（另加 People?）。S5a：「行銷組合必須支持策略」——要做到，不只是說。

    Strategic Marketing · Quick sheet

    Marketing on one line

    Understand → Choose → Do. Every framework from S1–S6 sits somewhere on this line, and each card gives one sentence you can adapt in the exam.

    Practice mode blurs the example sentences. Try saying one yourself, then click to compare.

    The one line

    The terms feel blurry when you learn them as a long list. Put them on one line and most of them have an obvious place. Dr. Shannon’s S1 slide says it directly: “The 5Cs, STP and the 4Ps constitute the marketing management framework.”

    Step 1 · Understand5CsWhat is going on? Who are we, who are the customers, who else is there?
    Step 2 · ChooseSTPWho do we serve, and why should they pick us over the others?
    Step 3 · Do4PsWhat do we sell, at what price, where, and how do we tell people?

    一條線就好：先了解狀況（5C）→ 再選要服務誰、要讓他們怎麼看我們（STP）→ 最後決定怎麼做（4P）。其他所有框架都是幫其中一步做得更好的工具。順序不能跳：還沒選好目標客群就開始想打折、打廣告，是老師最常扣分的地方。

    ExampleA coffee shop. Understand: office workers nearby, three chains already on the street, rent rising. Choose: target people who work from cafés; position as the quiet place with good Wi-Fi. Do: long tables and sockets (Product), a day pass (Price), stay near co-working offices (Place), posts on work-from-café pages (Promotion).一間咖啡店走一次：了解（附近上班族、街上已有三家連鎖、租金上漲）→ 選擇（鎖定在咖啡店工作的人，定位成安靜、網路好的地方）→ 執行（長桌插座、一日票、開在共享辦公室旁、在相關社群推廣）。

    Which step is the question asking about?

    Before picking any framework, read the question and decide which step it sits in. The words usually give it away.

    If the question says…StepReach for
    analyse the situation · compare two countries · is this market attractive · what is changing · who are the competitors · how do customers decide · what research would you do1 · Understand5Cs, SWOT, macro environment, market size/age/income, buyer decision process, competition, research methods
    who should they target · how should they position · sub-brand · move up- or down-market · enter a new country · grow · which products to keep2 · ChooseSegmentation, targeting strategy, positioning, perceptual map, growth grid (Ansoff), BCG, mission
    how to launch · how to increase sales · which channel · online or stores · how to promote · loyalty program3 · Do4Ps, funnel, CRM / lifetime value, e-commerce and omnichannel, disintermediation, new users / new uses / more usage

    看題目的關鍵字分到哪一步，就知道從哪一組框架裡挑。一題通常只需要一到兩個框架，有時候第一句先用 5C 交代狀況，再用另一個框架回答真正的問題。

    Before the line: the mindset

    These are less a step and more the way of thinking behind the whole line.

    Needs, wants, demandsS1

    Answers: Will people actually buy?

    Need: a felt lackWant: shaped by cultureDemand: want + money

    A large population is not enough on its own. Wants only become demand when people can pay, so income matters as much as size.

    人多不代表需求大，要有購買力。

    Marketing philosophiesS1

    Answers: What does the company put first?

    ProductionProductSellingMarketingSocietalStrategic

    The societal marketing concept asks a firm to meet customer wants while also protecting people’s long-term well-being.

    六種經營觀念；問到 CSR、健康、道德，就點名 societal marketing concept。

    Marketing myopia · USPS1

    Answers: Are we selling a feature or a benefit?

    Benefit, not featureUSP must be wantedFeatures get copied → brand

    Customers buy the benefit, not the feature. Most features can be copied, so the brand has to carry the difference.

    顧客買的是好處不是功能；功能會被抄，所以要靠品牌。

    Step 1 · Understand

    Everything here describes the situation. Nothing here is a decision yet.

    5CsS1 · S5a

    Answers: What is the situation?

    CompanyCustomersCompetitorsCollaboratorsContext

    Before choosing a strategy, I use the 5Cs to understand the situation: what the company has, what customers want, who else competes, who helps deliver, and what is changing around them.

    公司有什麼、顧客要什麼、還有誰在競爭、誰幫忙交付（供應商、通路）、外在環境在變什麼。

    SWOTS4a

    Answers: What do we have, and what is out there?

    S, W = inside the companyO, T = outside

    Rising health awareness is an opportunity, not a strength, because it happens outside the company.

    S、W 是公司內部、自己控制得了的；O、T 是外部的。最常錯：把市場趨勢寫成優勢。

    Macro environmentS4a

    Answers: What is changing around everyone?

    DemographicEconomicNaturalTechnologicalPoliticalCultural

    A proposed sugar tax is a political factor. It becomes a real threat only if it is passed.

    六個外在力量。注意「提案」還不是「法規」。

    Market size · age · incomeS1

    Answers: How big and how attractive is this market, and for whom?

    Population sizeAge profileIncomeGrowth

    Compared with Vietnam, Thailand has an older population but higher incomes, so it suits premium products more than mass-volume ones.

    比兩個市場要用同一組欄位並排比，不要只描述一國。

    Buyer decision processS4b

    Answers: How does the customer decide?

    Need recognitionInformation searchEvaluationPurchasePost-purchaseInvolvement · risk

    Buying a car is high involvement, so customers search and compare for a long time. A snack is low involvement and is often bought by habit.

    五個階段不是每次都走完；看涉入程度和風險。

    Utilitarian vs hedonicS4b

    Answers: Why do they buy?

    Utilitarian: useful, practicalHedonic: fun, pleasure

    People buy an energy drink for a utilitarian reason, to stay awake, but the brand can also add a hedonic reason, the feeling of being tough.

    實用動機 vs 享樂動機，常常同時存在。

    Direct vs indirect competitionS5b

    Answers: Who else could the customer choose?

    Direct: same categoryIndirect: different product, same need

    Other bubble-tea shops are direct competitors. A convenience-store iced coffee is an indirect competitor, because it meets the same need with a different product.

    同類商品 = 直接；不同商品但滿足同一個需要 = 間接。

    Market researchS3 · S2

    Answers: What do we need to find out, and how?

    Qualitative: why, howQuantitative: how many, how oftenObservation · experimentData mining: patterns, not reasons

    To learn why customers stopped coming, I would use in-depth interviews. To measure how many customers feel this way, I would use a survey.

    先講需要什麼資訊，再選方法。問「為什麼」用質化，問「多少」用量化；data mining 只看得到發生什麼，看不到原因。

    Step 2 · Choose

    This is where the real decisions are. Most strong answers spend their time here.

    SegmentationS4b · S5a

    Answers: How can we split the market into groups?

    Demographic: age, incomePsychographic: lifestyle, valuesGood segment: measurable, accessible, substantial, actionable

    Segmenting only by age misses the point here. Two 25-year-olds can want very different things, so I would segment by lifestyle as well.

    人口統計（年齡、收入）vs 心理統計（生活型態、價值觀）。好的區隔要量得到、接觸得到、夠大、做得出行動。

    Targeting strategyS5a

    Answers: How many groups do we go after?

    Undifferentiated: one offer for allDifferentiated: different offers per groupConcentrated: one niche

    With a small budget, a concentrated strategy makes sense: serve one niche well instead of competing with large chains for everyone.

    全部一起打、分群打、還是只打一個利基。

    Positioning · perceptual mapS5a

    Answers: Where do we sit in customers’ minds compared with rivals?

    Two axes customers care aboutPlot every brandLook for an empty space

    I would draw a perceptual map with “price” and “healthiness” as the axes. The upper-left corner, healthy but affordable, is empty, so that is where the brand should position.

    兩個軸要是顧客在乎、而且彼此不重複的特質（價格和品質常常是同一件事，別這樣配）。

    Growth grid (often called Ansoff)S4a

    Answers: Where should we grow?

    Penetration: same product, same marketProduct developmentMarket developmentDiversification

    Opening stores in Vietnam with the same menu is market development. It is riskier than penetration because the customers are new to us.

    S4a 投影片有這張表但沒寫名字，Ansoff 是教科書的叫法。越遠離熟悉的產品和市場，風險越高。

    BCG matrixS4a

    Answers: Which products or units do we invest in?

    StarQuestion markCash cowDog

    The original drink is a cash cow: high share in a slow-growing market. Its cash can fund the new product line, which is still a question mark.

    依市場成長率和相對市占分四格；問號不會自動變明星。

    Mission · brand consistencyS4a

    Answers: Does this choice still fit who we are?

    What is our business?Who are our customers?Every touchpoint tells the same story

    A sudden move to luxury would confuse current customers, because it does not match the brand’s mission of affordable everyday food.

    改目標或形象之前，先看跟使命、原本顧客合不合。

    Ethics of targetingS5a

    Answers: Is it fair to aim this at them?

    Who is targetedIs the product good for them?

    Targeting sugary drinks at children is likely to draw criticism, because the group is vulnerable and the product harms their health.

    鎖定弱勢族群推銷不健康產品，會被批評。

    Step 3 · Do

    Only now do the tactics come in. Each one should follow from the target and position you chose in step 2.

    4Ps (+ People)S1 · S5a

    Answers: How do we deliver and communicate the position?

    ProductPricePlacePromotion(People)

    Because the target is busy office workers who value speed, the 4Ps should all support convenience: a small menu, mid-range price, locations near offices and promotion through delivery apps.

    4P 要跟著目標客群和定位走，不能是一份放到哪都適用的清單。

    Marketing funnelS1

    Answers: Where are customers dropping out?

    AwarenessInterestTrialRepeat

    The campaign got many views, but views are only awareness. The real problem is that few people moved on to trial.

    曝光不等於銷售；每一層都要有自己的目標和衡量方式。

    CRM · lifetime valueS2

    Answers: How do we keep customers longer?

    Lifetime valueChurnLoyalty program goals

    A loyalty program should raise lifetime value, not just give discounts. I would measure repeat visits and churn.

    重點是長期關係與顧客終身價值；打折不等於關係。

    E-commerce · omnichannelS2

    Answers: Online, stores, or both?

    Bricks & mortarClicks & mortarPure playOne seamless experience

    For a product customers like to try first, stores still matter. Omnichannel lets them check stock online and pick up in store.

    要連到產品與顧客講，不能只說「線上是趨勢」。

    DisintermediationS5b

    Answers: Should we cut out the middleman?

    Control vs expertiseChannel conflict

    Selling through its own app gives the brand control and customer data, but it loses the reach of delivery platforms and may upset partners.

    自己賣能拿到控制權和資料，但要講失去了什麼。

    New users · new uses · more usageS5b

    Answers: Where can more sales come from?

    New usersNew usesMore usage

    The brand can find new users (older adults), new uses (as a cooking ingredient) or more usage (a larger family pack).

    三個來源，給兩個點子時別用同一個來源。

    DownsizingS5b

    Answers: Is a smaller pack at the same price fair?

    Same price, less productUnit price risesHard to compare

    Shrinking the pack from 500 g to 450 g at the same price is a hidden price rise of about 11%, which customers may see as deceptive.

    縮量不漲價，單位價格其實上升；少了 10% 的量，單價漲了約 11%。

    Pairs that get mixed up

    These twoThe difference in one line
    5Cs vs SWOT5Cs describe the situation. SWOT sorts what you found into inside/outside so you can make a decision. Use one or the other in a table, not both mixed together.
    5Cs vs growth grid5Cs is step 1 (what is going on). The growth grid is step 2 (which product–market to go for). The grid uses what the 5Cs found.
    Growth grid vs STPThe grid chooses which market to grow into. STP chooses who inside a market to serve and how to stand out to them.
    4Ps vs funnel4Ps are the tools you control. The funnel is the customer’s steps, which you measure to see whether the tools work.
    Segmentation vs targetingSegmentation splits the market. Targeting picks which pieces to serve.
    Direct vs indirect competitorSame category = direct. Different product, same need = indirect.

    之前混淆過的 Ansoff：它不是 5C，也不是顧客端或賣方端的分析，而是第 2 步「選擇」裡面決定「往哪裡成長」的工具，用的是 5C 查到的資訊。

    Try it yourself

    Pick a brand you know well. Do it in Chinese first, then write one English sentence per step using the lines above.

    1. Understand: write one line each for the 5Cs.
    2. Choose: name one target group and finish this sentence: “For [target], [brand] is the [position] because [reason].”
    3. Do: one 4P decision for each P, and check that each one fits the target.
    4. Look back: could you jump from step 1 straight to step 3? If yes, step 2 is missing.

    用你熟的品牌，先用中文走一次三步，再每一步寫一句英文。如果你發現自己從第 1 步直接跳到 4P，就是漏了第 2 步。
